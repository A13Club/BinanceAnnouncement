How It Works: 
Step 1: Build Your Sub-Affiliate Team
An Affiliate can invite Sub-Affiliates to join their team and share a portion of their own commission rate to build the Sub-Affiliate team. Please refer to this FAQ for how to participate in the Binance Sub-Affiliate Feature.
Step 2: Sub-Affiliates Refer New Users
Sub-Affiliates invite new users to register during the Promotion Period, complete account verification (KYC), and make deposits and trading.
Step 3: Reward Distribution
After the Promotion Period ends, Affiliates could qualify for a reward of up to 5,000 USDC based on the achievement of each of the following three assessment metrics.
Challenge Tiers and Rewards:
Affiliates who meet all three metrics in any one challenge tier will qualify for the corresponding reward. Rewards are granted at the highest tier achieved and are not cumulative.
