Binance is excited to launch the USDC Holding APR Promotion! During the Promotion Period, all eligible users will enjoy a 2% APR reward on their eligible Net USDC Holding in Binance Futures and Portfolio Margin accounts. 
Promotion Period: 2026-10-27 00:00 (UTC) to 2026-11-23 23:59 (UTC)
Promotion Mechanism
1. Eligible Users
To earn rewards for a weekly period, users must generate at least $2,500 in USDC-margined trading volume (USDC-M Futures pairs) within that weekly period. The Promotion Period consists of four weekly periods:
Weekly PeriodTime Frame (UTC)Week 12026-10-27 00:00 - 2026-11-02 23:59 Week 22026-11-03 00:00 - 2026-11-09 23:59 Week 32026-11-10 00:00 - 2026-11-16 23:59Week 42026-11-17 00:00 - 2026-11-23 23:59 
2. Eligible Asset
USDC balances as defined under "Net USDC Holding" below: Futures account USDC balance for all users, plus Margin account USDC balance for Portfolio Margin users.
3. Eligible Balance
Net USDC Holding: The maximum eligible balance is $1,000,000 Net USDC Holding per user, applied at the master account level. Net USDC Holding across all sub-accounts will be aggregated under the master account. Any amount above $1,000,000 will not earn rewards. 
Net USDC Holding is calculated as:
Portfolio Margin users: Portfolio Margin account USDC balance  − Portfolio Margin account USDC liability Non-Portfolio Margin users: Futures account USDC balance − Margin account USDC liability 
Notes: 
Portfolio Margin: A unified margin mode that lets users share collateral across UM + CM + Margin accounts. For more information, refer to this Portfolio Margin FAQ.Margin account USDC liability refers to the total outstanding borrowed USDC in the Margin account.Negative Net USDC Holding are not eligible to receive rewards.
4. Rewards Calculation & Distribution
Daily reward = min(daily average Net USDC Holding, $1,000,000) × 2% ÷ 365Net USDC Holding is measured via snapshots taken at random times between 00:00:00 and 23:59:59 (UTC) each dayRewards accrue daily and are settled weekly: Users who meet the weekly trading volume threshold will receive all rewards accrued during that weekly period. For these purposes, the daily average Net USDC Holding is the average of the Net USDC Holding recorded across that day’s snapshots.Rewards will be distributed in USDC to the eligible user’s Spot account  (master account) within 5 calendar days (excluding weekends and UAE public holidays) after each weekly period ends.
Example: User A maintains a daily average Net USDC Holding of $1,500,000 during Week 1 and generates $3,000 in USDC-margined trading volume in Week 1. User A will receive ($1,000,000 × 2% ÷ 365) × 7 ≈ $383.56 in USDC for Week 1.
