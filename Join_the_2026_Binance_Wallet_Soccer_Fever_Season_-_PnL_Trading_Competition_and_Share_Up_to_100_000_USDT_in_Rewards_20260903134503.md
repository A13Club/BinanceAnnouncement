Trading Score Calculation Details:

Leaderboard rankings are based on Trading Score, not raw realized PnL.

Realized PnL of an eligible market = PnL from buying and selling Prediction Markets outcome shares + Profits claimed from settled markets

Trading Score from an eligible market = sign(Realized PnL of that market) * √|Realized PnL of that market|

Total Trading Score = Sum of Trading Score from all eligible markets

Aggregation by market: All orders within the same market are first totaled into that market's realized PnL, before taking the square root.Sign is preserved: A profitable market produces a positive Trading Score; a losing market produces a negative one.Precision: Both the Trading Score from each market and the total Trading Score are kept to 2 decimal places, truncated (not rounded) from the 3rd decimal onward.Eligibility threshold: A total Trading Score above 0.00 is required to qualify for rewards. If two or more participants’ total Trading Scores are equal, the participant who registered earlier ranks higher.Refresh and settlement: Trading Score is updated hourly and may rise or fall.Eligible markets: Markets under the "Soccer" category with a settlement time that falls within the Competition Period.

Example:Suppose a user spends 40 USDT to buy 100 outcome shares of Option A at 0.40 USDT per share. The price later rises to 0.70, and the user sells 50 shares for 35 USDT. The user holds the remaining 50 shares through settlement, wins, and claims 50 USDT (1 USDT per winning share).

Realized PnL for this market = 35 + 50 - 40 = 45 USDTTrading Score from this market = √45 = 6.70

Note: This formula is a simplified example. Actual calculations are subject to the full competition rules.
