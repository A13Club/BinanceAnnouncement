# Binance Will Discontinue Mainnet Support of BounceBit (BB)

This is a general announcement and marketing communication. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

Following the hacking incident on BounceBit, the project has decided to permanently sunset the BounceBit Chain. As such, Binance will discontinue the mainnet support of BounceBit (BB), as well as open deposits and withdrawals via the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network for BounceBit (BB).

General

Deposits and WithdrawalsAt 2026-08-20 02:00 (UTC), deposits and withdrawals of the aforementioned token via the BounceBit mainnet were suspended.At 2026-09-01 07:00 (UTC), deposits and withdrawals of the BB token via the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network will be opened.Deposits and withdrawals will no longer be supported on the BounceBit mainnet.Spot, Margin, Futures trading and Binance Earn services will not be impacted during the contract swap.Binance will handle all technical requirements for users who are involved in this event.

Contract Swap

BounceBit (BB) will be migrated from its mainnet to BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network at a ratio of 1:1.

New BB token smart contract address: BB (BEP20)
