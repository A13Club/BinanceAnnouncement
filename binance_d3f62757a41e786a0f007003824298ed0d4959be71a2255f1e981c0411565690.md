Fellow Binancians,
We recognize the past few days have been difficult. In appreciation of your trust and patience, Binance is introducing the limited time VIP Level Protection Program for eligible VIP users. The program provides eligible VIP 1-9 users with 30 days of VIP level protection during the Validity Period below on a one-off discretionary basis.
Eligibility
All users who reached VIP 1-9 at any point between 2026-10-21 00:00 (UTC) and 2026-10-30 23:59 (UTC) are considered "eligible users" of this program, including:
Users in the VIP ProgramUsers currently under existing VIP loyalty programUsers who have applied a valid VIP Level Upgrade Trial Voucher which is subsequently approved
30-Day VIP Level Protection
Binance will set the highest VIP level the user reached between 2026-10-21 00:00 (UTC) and 2026-10-30 23:59 (UTC) as the "Protected Level".For the 30-day period from 2026-11-03 00:00 (UTC) to 2026-12-02 23:59 (UTC) (“Validity Period”), the user's VIP level will not fall below the Protected Level regardless of whether the user meets the usual requirements under the VIP program.Changes to a user's VIP level above the Protected Level are unaffected. If a user reaches a higher VIP level through assets or trading volume, they will continue to enjoy the benefits of that higher level under the platform's current VIP rules.Throughout the Validity Period, users continue to enjoy all exclusive benefits and services corresponding to their current VIP level, ensuring an uninterrupted benefits experience.
