# Binance Launches Limited-Time Spot Taker Program Promotion (2026-11-09)

This is a general announcement and marketing communication. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.
Fellow Binancians,
As Binance conducts regular reviews of its programs to provide users with the most value and competitive services, Binance is launching a limited-time Spot Taker Program Promotion, effective from 2026-11-09 00:00 (UTC).
Key Highlights:
The promotion introduces three discount tiers — 5%, 10% and 15% — with the entry threshold set at a weekly Spot taker volume share of 0.05%.The promotion covers USDT-quoted and USDC-quoted Spot pairs. Both quote assets are assessed together under a single track, and the discount is distributed separately for each quote asset.No application or enrollment is required. Users who meet the qualification criteria are automatically identified and rewarded.
Promotion Period: 2026-11-09 00:00 (UTC) to 2027-01-03 23:59 (UTC).
The Binance Spot Taker Program Promotion is as follows:
Fee TierWeekly Spot Taker Volume Share on USDT-Quoted and USDC-Quoted Spot Pairs Taker Fee Discount10.05% ≤ X < 0.2%5%20.2% ≤ X < 1.5%10%3X ≥ 1.5%15%
Please Note:
No application or enrollment is required. Users who meet the qualification criteria in a given assessment week will be automatically identified and included in the promotion.The taker fee discount will not be applied directly to trades. Instead, it will be distributed to the Spot Account of the user's master account before the Friday of the week following the discount period:Taker fees paid on USDT-quoted pairs are returned as a USDT distribution;Taker fees paid on USDC-quoted pairs are returned as a USDC distribution.The first assessment week of this promotion will run from 2026-11-02 00:00 (UTC) to 2026-11-08 23:59 (UTC). The taker fee discount based on this first assessment week will be applied during 2026-11-10 00:00 (UTC) to 2026-11-16 23:59 (UTC). The discount generated during this period will be credited to eligible users' master accounts' Spot Accounts before 2026-11-20 23:59 (UTC). Subsequent assessment weeks will follow the same weekly cycle, with the applicable discount period and credit timing set out in the example above.Please note that in special circumstances, the distribution may be delayed.Distribution amount = Taker fees actually paid during the assessment week * discount rateUSDT distribution = Taker fees actually paid (USDT-quoted pairs) * discount rateUSDC distribution = Taker fees actually paid (USDC-quoted pairs) * discount rateTaker fees actually paid = Taker fees charged to the user after their VIP tier rates, USDC-pair discount and any BNB discount have been appliedTrading volume and taker fees generated by sub-accounts will be aggregated at the master account level for the purpose of tier assessment and distribution.No daily or weekly report will be provided for the Spot Taker Program Promotion. Users may refer to their account transaction records for the distributed rewards.Trading volume and fees on Spot and Margin pairs under zero fee promotions (i.e., where zero maker and taker fees apply) will be excluded from calculation.
For more information and further inquiries, users can email mmprogram@binance.com or contact their sales and key account coverage teams directly.
