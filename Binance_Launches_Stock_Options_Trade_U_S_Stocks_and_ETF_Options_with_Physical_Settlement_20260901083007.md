# Binance Launches Stock Options — Trade U.S. Stocks and ETF Options with Physical Settlement

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

Binance is bringing traditional markets to crypto-native trading with the launch of Stock Options. Stock Options are now live, offering physically-settled options contracts on U.S.-listed stocks and ETFs, giving users the right to buy (Call) or sell (Put) underlying shares at a predetermined strike price on a specific expiration date.

As part of Binance's growing TradFi product suite, Stock Options give traders a new way to express direction, hedge equity positions, and manage portfolio risk — all from a single Binance account. With Stock Options, users can trade bullish or bearish views on U.S. stocks and ETFs with defined risk: the maximum loss for option buyers is limited to the premium paid.

How to Start Trading Stock Options on Binance

Open an account: Complete the Options Suitability Quiz and sign the Disclaimer. If you haven't opened Stocks before, you can open Stocks and Options together in one step.Navigate to Options: Go to the Stock kline page and select the "Options" tab (between "Overview" and "Financials").Select expiration date and strike price: Choose from the available expiration dates and strike prices in the options chain.Place a limit order: Enter your limit price and quantity (contracts), then click [Buy]. Only limit orders are supported in Phase 1.Monitor your position: View your positions on the Stock Trading Page or in your Funding Account. Check Position Details for PnL, Greeks, and more.

Please refer to the FAQ for full guidance on how to get started with Stock Options trading.

Tip: To check whether a specific stock supports options trading, go to the Stock kline page and look for the "Options" tab. If the tab is present, options trading is available for that  underlying.

Trading Hours

Trading follows U.S. options market hours and may vary by product and exchange.

For most U.S. stock options:

Regular trading hours are 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM ET.Stock options generally do not support pre-market or post-market trading.

For certain ETF and ETN options:

Some ETF and ETN options are late-close exceptions and may trade until 4:15 PM ET.

During non-trading hours:

New orders are not accepted.Existing open orders remain on the book.Users are able to cancel open orders.Matching will not occur until the market reopens.

Trading may also be affected by U.S. market holidays, early closes, trading halts, or other exchange events. Users should refer to the trading interface for the applicable market status of each option.

Stock Options are contracts that give you the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell the underlying Securities at a specified price on or before a specified expiration date. You pay a Premium to acquire this right. Stock Options do not represent ownership of the underlying Securities. Stock Options can be exercised at any time before the cut-off time on the relevant expiration date. You are solely responsible for monitoring your open Stock Option positions and determining whether to exercise them. To exercise a Stock Option, you must submit an exercise instruction through the Binance Platform. If you do not submit an exercise instruction before the cut-off, your Stock Option will be subject to auto-liquidation, meaning that your position will be sold on a best-efforts basis before the close of trading. Your Stock Option may expire without value and you may lose the entire Premium paid. Even if your Stock Option is in-the-money at expiration, it will not be automatically exercised unless you have submitted an exercise instruction.

Stock Options are subject to high market and liquidity risk and price volatility. The value of your investment may go down or up and you may not get back the amount invested. Stock Options are only available for trading during regular market hours. You are solely responsible for your investment decisions and Binance is not liable for any losses you may incur.
