# Meet Binance AI: A Personalized Trading Experience for Every User

Fellow Binancians,

Binance is excited to officially introduce Binance AI, a new suite of features designed to bring adaptive, personalized financial experiences to our global platform. 

As markets evolve, Binance AI helps users navigate this complexity by offering clearer context, tailored layouts and tools that match their needs, goals and experience. By making complex market information easier to access and understand, Binance AI aims to broaden financial inclusion and lower barriers for everyone.

What Is Binance AI

Binance AI is built around three core principles to enhance users’ trading experience:

Adapts to the user: The experience changes based on personal needs. Newer users see simpler explanations and prompts, while active traders can access deeper market context, data views, and product-specific modules.Simplified market context: Binance AI surfaces relevant insights, news, and data tied to the assets, products, and market themes users follow.Full control: Customizable widgets let users choose what appears on the screen. The layout can be reconfigured with users’ preferred tools, market updates, and educational content at any time.

Key Features of Binance AI:

Adaptive and customizable UI: AI-generated layouts adapt modules and widgets to user needs. The interface changes based on the assets they’re exploring, from crypto to traditional finance. Examples of what might be displayed include:Yesterday's PNL: A recap of users’ previous day's performance alongside key events for the top holdings.Top movers: 24-hour price movements across users’ holdings, with AI-generated analysis personalized to what they hold and follow.Smart alerts: Voice alerts when users’ tracked assets move, so there’s no need to watch the screen.Smart tooltips: Contextual, AI-powered prompts that help users understand what they’re seeing and discover timely information. If a user is on a product page for the first time, tips will appear to guide them and help them understand how it works. Master trade: Users can explore profiles of selected investors and traders to understand how experienced market participants approach asset allocation. During periods of market uncertainty, this adds useful context on how others are positioned.Market brief: On-demand market updates in text or audio, refreshed every four hours. Users can stay informed on key developments without switching between multiple information sources.

How to Access Binance AI:

Binance AI is being progressively rolled out to all eligible users on the Binance App and website. To access it on the Binance App, simply update to the latest version, and the personalized features will automatically begin adapting to your usage.
