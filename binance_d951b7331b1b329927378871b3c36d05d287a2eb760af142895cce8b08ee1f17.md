# Binance Collaborates with Cboe to Bring Real-Time Market Benchmark Data to Users

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.
Fellow Binancians,
We're excited to announce that Binance has integrated the Cboe Global Indices Feed into the Binance platform, app and Binance AI, giving users access to trusted real-time traditional market data into the Binance experience.
Markets are increasingly interconnected. The feed gives users trusted TradFi benchmarks to compare against digital asset market activity and helps them better understand cross-market movements within a single platform. 
What's Available Now
The initial rollout covers three of the world's most widely followed U.S. equity benchmarks, with more benchmark data to follow:
S&P 500 Index (SPX®)Dow Jones Industrial Average (DJX®)Nasdaq-100 (NDX®)
Integration with Binance AI
Following the recent launch of Binance AI — a suite of AI-powered features bringing adaptive, personalized financial experiences — Binance AI will use this data to explain cross-market movements and highlight market context personalized to each user's interests and activity.
