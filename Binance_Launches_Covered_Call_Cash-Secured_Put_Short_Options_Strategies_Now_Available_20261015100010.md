What Are Covered Call and Cash-Secured Put:

Covered Call is a strategy where you hold shares of the underlying stock and sell a call option against those shares. You collect a premium upfront. If the option is assigned, your shares will be sold at the strike price. Your upside is limited to the strike price, while the premium offsets part of any downside loss.

Cash-Secured Put is a strategy where you sell a put option while setting aside the full cash amount needed to buy the shares if assigned. You collect a premium upfront. If the option is assigned, you will be required to buy the underlying shares at the strike price, even if the market price is lower. The premium offsets only part of the loss if the stock falls sharply.

Both strategies require collateral — shares for Covered Call, cash for Cash-Secured Put — which is frozen until the position is closed, the option expires, or the option is assigned.
