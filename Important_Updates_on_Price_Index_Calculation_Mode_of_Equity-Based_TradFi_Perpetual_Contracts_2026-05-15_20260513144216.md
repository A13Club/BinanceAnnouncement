# Important Updates on Price Index Calculation Mode of Equity-Based TradFi Perpetual Contracts (2026-05-15)

This is a general Binance Exchange Notice. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

Binance constantly reviews its product and service offerings to ensure the best user experience for all users. Effective from 2026-05-15 21:00 (UTC), Binance will introduce Orderbook EWMA price index calculation mode on equity-based TradFi perpetual contracts to replace Fixed Mode during the daily maintenance period, weekends, and holidays.

Notes: 

Orderbook EWMA price index calculation mode will be applied on all existing equity-based TradFi perpetual contracts and any new equity-based TradFi perpetual contracts to be listed on Binance Futures in the future.  This information is released as a Notice under Binance Clearing Procedures. In case there is a discrepancy between this notice and any Futures FAQs, please refer to this notice for the most accurate and updated information.
