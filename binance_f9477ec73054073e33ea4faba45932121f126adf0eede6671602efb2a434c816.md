# Binance Upgrades Spot Liquidity Provider Program (2026-11-16)

This is a general announcement and marketing communication. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.
Fellow Binancians,
As Binance conducts regular reviews of its programs to provide users with the most value and competitive services, Binance has upgraded its Spot Liquidity Provider Program with a limited-time promotion, effective from 2026-11-16 00:00 (UTC).
Key Highlights:
In addition to the existing Total Spot Volume track, Binance introduced two new qualification tracks for all tiers. Users may now qualify through their Weekly Spot Maker Volume % (BTC & ETH Pairs) or their Weekly Spot Maker Volume % (excluding that on BTC & ETH pairs).All tier thresholds remain unchanged. Users will be awarded the highest tier achieved on any single track. A user only needs to qualify on one track, there is no requirement to qualify on all of them.The first assessment week of the new tracks started from 2026-11-16 00:00 (UTC) to 2026-11-22 23:59 (UTC), with the corresponding maker fee rebates updated at approximately 2026-11-24 00:00 (UTC).
Promotion Period: 2026-11-16 00:00 (UTC) to 2027-02-07 23:59 (UTC)

bStocks Track:
A limited-time bStocks promotional track continues to run alongside the 3 tracks above. Important Notes:
Binance will provide daily and weekly reports to all participating liquidity providers, which contain details of their performances.Liquidity providers will be reviewed on a weekly basis (Monday 00:00 UTC to Sunday 23:59 UTC). Fee tier adjustments are automatically updated every Tuesday around 00:00 (UTC).The first assessment week of the new tracks started from 2026-11-16 00:00 (UTC) to 2026-11-22 23:59 (UTC), with the corresponding maker fee rebates updated at approximately 2026-11-24 00:00 (UTC).Self-trades will be excluded from maker volume calculations.All trading volume and metrics related to the promotion are measured by Binance at its sole and absolute discretion.
How to Participate:
Automatic Participation: Users with Weekly Maker Volume that equals or exceeds the Tier 1 requirements of the Binance Spot Liquidity Provider Program are automatically enrolled.Manual Application (trial period): Users whose current 30-day Maker Volume do not meet the Tier 1 requirements, but achieve a trading volume of over 20,000,000 USD equivalent on Binance Spot or over 100,000,000 USD equivalent on Binance Futures, may apply for a trial period of four weeks, where they will enjoy Tier 2 maker fee rebates during this period. Eligible users can email mmprogram@binance.com or contact their Key Account Coverage or sales team with their proof of trading volume to apply for the trial.
Qualifications:
A. To qualify by weekly Spot maker volume over the total Binance Spot maker volume
Weekly Spot maker volume % = (Spot maker volume over the week / total Binance Spot maker volume over the week) * 100%At the end of each week, the weekly Spot maker volume % for each participant will be calculated, and maker fee rebates are awarded to eligible participants whose weekly Spot maker volume % is equal to or above 0.05%.
B. To qualify by weekly Spot maker volume on BTC & ETH pairs over the total Binance Spot maker volume on BTC & ETH pairs
Weekly Spot maker volume % (BTC & ETH pairs) = (Spot maker volume over the week (BTC & ETH pairs) / total Binance Spot maker volume over the week (BTC & ETH pairs)) * 100%At the end of each week, the weekly Spot maker volume % (BTC & ETH pairs) for each participant will be calculated, and maker fee rebates are awarded to eligible participants whose weekly Spot maker volume % (BTC & ETH pairs) is equal to or above 0.05%.BTC & ETH pairs are pairs where the non-stablecoin asset is BTC or ETH. For example, BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT, BTC/USDC, ETH/BTC and FDUSD/BTC are all BTC & ETH pairs.
C. To qualify by weekly Spot maker volume on Excluding that on BTC and ETH Pairs over the total Binance Spot maker volume on Excluding that on BTC and ETH Pairs pairs
Weekly Spot maker volume % (excluding that on BTC & ETH pairs) = (Spot maker volume over the week (Excluding that on BTC & ETH pairs) / total Binance Spot maker volume over the week (excluding that on BTC & ETH pairs)) * 100%.At the end of each week, the weekly Spot maker volume % (excluding that on BTC & ETH pairs) for each participant will be calculated, and maker fee rebates are awarded to eligible participants whose weekly Spot maker volume % (excluding that on BTC & ETH pairs) is equal to or above 0.05%.
