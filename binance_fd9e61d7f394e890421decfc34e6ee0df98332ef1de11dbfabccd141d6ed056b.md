# Important: Transactions with Certain Crypto Platforms

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.
Fellow Binancians,
Following the recent FinCEN designation of MEXC as a Primary Money Laundering Concern, Binance will no longer be in position to process transactions involving crypto-asset service providers/platforms included in the above designation, as set out below. 
MEXC Global LimitedMEXC Estonia OÜGIDA Technology AGMXC Global LtdMEXC Global Foundation LtdMEXC Singapore Pte LtdMEXC (Comoros) LtdMEXCMEXC GlobalMEX Global LtdMX Global LTDMEX Exchange (former name)MEXC affiliates
Accordingly, please do not directly or indirectly send to, receive from, or otherwise engage in transactions through Binance involving these entities. 
Any requests to send funds from a Binance aAccountwallet to any of the above entities will be declined.  Any deposits received by Binance from any of the above entities will be temporarily withheld and subject to compliance review and users may potentially be subject to further restriction in accordance with Binance Terms of Use, including offboarding.  During the compliance review, restrictions may also be applied to the impacted account as such activity may also constitute a breach of Binance Terms of Use. 
If you have any questions, please feel free to contact Binance Support, available 24/7.
