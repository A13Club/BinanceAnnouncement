# Notice on Share Count Basis of USDⓈ-Margined ANTHROPICUSDT Pre-IPO Perp

Fellow Binancians,
ANTHROPICUSDT Pre-IPO Perp is currently quoted on an estimated share count of 1 billion shares (as specified in the Contract Specifications published on 2026-06-02) until the contract size is adjusted.
On 2026-11-10, Anthropic published its S-1/A-2, which disclosed the anticipated share count in connection with its IPO.  
An adjustment to the Contract Size will be announced prior to the Official IPO Listing date. However, the exact timing of the Contract Size adjustment and details will be announced separately, including by way of Exchange Notice under the Exchange Rules. No change will be made to the current Contract Size of the ANTHROPICUSDT Pre-IPO Perp until such further notice.
Based on Anthropic’s S-1/A-2 filing, the estimated share count is expected to be revised to 13,116,075,288 shares, constituted as set out below:

The Underlying Asset of ANTHROPICUSDT Pre-IPO Perp is the price of 1 common share of Anthropic, PBC (“Anthropic”). The share count basis is common shares issued and outstanding. Unexercised options, unsettled RSUs, unconverted convertibles, shares issuable upon exercise of the underwriters' option to purchase additional shares, and shares reserved for future issuance (not yet granted) under equity plans are not yet common shares and are not included. For the avoidance of doubt, the expected revised estimated share count is the total of all classes of common shares shown on the cover of the S-1/A-2 as outstanding after the offering and the concurrent private placement, plus the common shares disclosed in the S-1/A-2 as issued after 2026-09-30 in connection with acquisitions of privately-held companies and in the Matching Issuance.
Binance Futures may further revise the estimated share count if the issuer updates the filing before Official IPO Listing. The actual share count may be different. 
Change of Ticker
Binance Futures will rename ANTHROPICUSDT Pre-IPO Perp to ANTUSDT Pre-IPO Perp during contract size adjustment. The ticker rename will not impact open positions; users are not required to close or settle their positions as a result of the contract size adjustment process.
