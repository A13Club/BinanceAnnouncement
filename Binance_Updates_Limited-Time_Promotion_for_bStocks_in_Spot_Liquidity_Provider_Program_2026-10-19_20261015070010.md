How the Rule Applies

A participant's bStocks tier is determined by their Weekly Maker Volume percentage on bStocks. When a participant reaches a bStocks tier, the corresponding maker fee rebate is applied across all symbols traded by that participant.

Example: A market maker (MM) whose original category tier is Tier 1 (based on the existing Spot program's qualification), and who through bStocks volume achieves bStocks Tier 3 (Weekly Maker Volume percentage ≥ 0.50%), will receive the Tier 3 maker fee rebate (−0.0060%) applied to all symbols.

How to Participate

Automatic Participation: Users with Weekly Maker Volume that equals or exceeds Tier 1 requirements of the Binance Spot Liquidity Provider Program are automatically enrolled.Manual Application (trial period): Users whose current 30-day Maker Volume has not met Tier 1 requirements, but has a trading volume of over 20,000,000 USD equivalent on Binance Spot market can apply for a trial period of four weeks, where the users will enjoy Tier 1 maker fee rebates during this period. Please email mmprogram@binance.com or contact your Key Account Coverage or sales to provide proof to apply for the trial.
