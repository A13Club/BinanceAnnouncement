# Binance Will Support the Cash Dividend Distribution of The Wendy's Company (WEN), NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA), Qualcomm Incorporated (QCOM), PayPal Holdings, Inc. (PYPL), and Western Digital Corporation (WDC) via bStocks

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. bStocks are offered through an Approved Prospectus in the ADGM and are not offered in any other jurisdiction. 
bStocks are Tokenized securities issued by BTech Holdings Limited, a Binance group affiliate. bStocks are classified as Certificates representing certain Financial Instruments (paragraph 92, Schedule 1 to FSMR). bStocks represent an interest in underlying securities held by the Issuer and are not direct ownership of the underlying Shares. bStocks do not allow holders to directly own a share or stock in the underlying listed company.
Fellow Binancians,
Binance will support The Wendy's Company (WEN), NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA), Qualcomm Incorporated (QCOM), PayPal Holdings, Inc. (PYPL), and Western Digital Corporation (WDC) cash dividend distribution via bStocks for WENB, NVDAB, QCOMB, PYPLB, and WDCB holders. Net amount of the cash dividend will be reinvested after applicable withholding taxes, fees, costs, and other deductions, into additional units or fractions of the same underlying security. Eligible users will receive dividends in the form of WENB, NVDAB, QCOMB, PYPLB and WDCB bStocks respectively.
On-chain holders of WENB, NVDAB, QCOMB, PYPLB and WDCB will receive the bStock dividends through a multiplier adjustment.
Eligibility
Users with WENB holdings on the record date snapshot at 2026-12-01 01:00 (UTC) will be eligible for the dividend distribution.Users with NVDAB holdings on the record date snapshot at 2026-12-02 01:00 (UTC) will be eligible for the dividend distribution.Users with QCOMB holdings on the record date snapshot at 2026-12-03 01:00 (UTC) will be eligible for the dividend distribution.Users with PYPLB and WDCB holdings on the record date snapshot at 2026-12-04 01:00 (UTC) will be eligible for the dividend distribution.
