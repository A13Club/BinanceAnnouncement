# Important Updates on Dividend Adjustment Process of SKHYNIXUSDT and SKHYUSDT Perpetual Contracts

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.
Fellow Binancians,
A dividend of 20,000 KRW per share has been declared by SK hynix Inc. on its common shares, which are listed on the KOSPI Market of the Korea Exchange under ticker 000660, according to the issuer’s announcement. A dividend adjustment will be applied to the SKHYNIXUSDT perpetual contract. The dividend adjustment amount is not capped by the standard funding rate cap or floor, so users are advised to assess the dividend adjustment amount and manage their positions accordingly before the ex-dividend date, 2026-11-27 08:00 (Korea Standard Time).SKHY is the NASDAQ-listed American Depositary Receipt (ADR) of SK hynix Inc. and each SKHY ADR represents one-tenth (0.1) of a common share of SK hynix Inc. listed on the KOSPI Market of the Korea Exchange under ticker 000660. The dividend attributable to each SKHY ADR is therefore 2,000 KRW, equivalent to 1.49 USD based on an exchange rate of 1,341.92 KRW per USD. The ex-dividend date for SKHY ADRs is the record date, based on the depositary’s confirmation in respect of previous dividends. The record date is 2026-11-30, as disclosed in the issuer’s Form 6-K. As the SKHYUSDT perpetual contract is USDT-priced, a real-time USDT/USD rate provided by third-party vendors will be applied to the 1.49 USD dividend adjustment amount per SKHY when the adjustment is determined on one calendar day before the record date, 2026-11-29 20:00 (Eastern Time). The final dividend adjustment amount may differ from 1.49 USD per SKHY.
Notes:
The dividend adjustment amount is applied as a Special Dividend Funding Amount under Procedure 73A of the Clearing Procedures and is not capped by the standard funding cap/floor. The dividend adjustment amount could therefore be higher than the funding cap/floor.
