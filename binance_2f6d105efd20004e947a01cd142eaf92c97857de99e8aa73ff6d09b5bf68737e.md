Tournament Rules
1. Accounts and funds
The organizers will provide standardized tournament accounts, each with an initial balance of 10,000 USDT, ensuring that all traders start under the same conditions.
2. Trading scope
The tournament will use Binance Futures and is limited to USDT trading pairs. Within that scope, there are no restrictions on which cryptocurrencies may be traded. Leverage of up to 150x is available, subject to the limits applicable to each trading pair and the platform.
3. Tournament duration
The trading tournament will last 90 minutes. All traders will start and finish trading at the same time, with PnL calculated in real time.
4. Rankings
Traders will be ranked by their account PnL amounts at the end of the tournament. The trader with the highest PnL will be named champion. Final rankings are subject to official verification and announcement by the tournament organizers.
5. Tournament restrictions
Withdrawals, transfers and the use of external APIs are prohibited during the tournament. Traders who violate the rules will be disqualified.
