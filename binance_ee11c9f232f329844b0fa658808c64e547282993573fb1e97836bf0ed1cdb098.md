# Binance Will Support Sandisk Corporation (SNDK) 8-for-1 Forward Stock Split via bStocks

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. bStocks are offered through an Approved Prospectus in the ADGM and are not offered in any other jurisdiction. 
bStocks are Tokenized securities issued by BTech Holdings Limited, a Binance group affiliate. bStocks are classified as Certificates representing certain Financial Instruments (paragraph 92, Schedule 1 to FSMR). bStocks represent an interest in underlying securities held by the Issuer and are not direct ownership of the underlying Shares. bStocks do not allow holders to directly own a share or stock in the underlying listed company.
Fellow Binancians,
Binance will support the Sandisk Corporation (SNDK) 8-for-1 forward stock split via bStocks for SNDKB holders. Each eligible SNDKB holder will receive 7 additional SNDKB for every 1 SNDKB held, for a total of 8 SNDKB per pre-split token. On-chain holders of SNDKB will receive the additional bStocks through a multiplier adjustment. When trading resumes, the SNDKB price will reflect the split-adjusted price of approximately 1/8 of the pre-split price.

Spot
At 2026-11-20 00:30 (UTC), Binance will suspend trading on the SNDKB/USDT spot trading pair. All pending orders will be automatically canceled after trading is suspended.Binance will suspend Trading Bots services for the SNDKB/USDT spot trading pair at 2026-11-20 00:30 (UTC) where applicable. Users are strongly advised to update and/or cancel their Trading Bots prior to this time to avoid any potential losses.Trading for the SNDKB/USDT spot trading pair will resume at the split-adjusted price at 2026-11-20 09:00 (UTC).
Margin
At 2026-11-18 15:00 (UTC), Binance Margin will remove SNDKB as eligible collateral. The cross and portfolio margin pair(s) of SNDKB will also be removed from Margin. Effective immediately, users will no longer be able to transfer any amount of SNDKB via manual transfers and Auto-Transfer Mode for Cross and Portfolio Margin into their margin accounts.If users hold collateral of SNDKB on cross or portfolio margin, it will be automatically liquidated and converted to USDT or USDC at a rate quoted by Binance Convert, and the proceeds will be added to the Margin Account balance. In the situation that the conversion fails, the SNDKB collateral will be dealt with in accordance with Binance Margin's standard liquidation process. The delisting ratio will apply. Binance is not liable for any losses incurred.Cross Margin or Portfolio Margin users are advised to transfer SNDKB out of their Margin Accounts to their Spot Accounts and to top up their margin balance before Margin Delisting Time where applicable. Users should monitor the Margin Level (ML) or Unified Maintenance Margin Ratio (uniMMR) closely to avoid any potential liquidation that may result from the removal of SNDKB from the Margin Account.Please note: For Futures Perpetual Contracts, please refer to Binance Futures Will Adjust the Contract Size of USDⓈ-Margined SNDKUSDT Perpetual Contract (2026-11-20).Please note that users will not be able to update their positions during the removal process, which may take approximately 3 hours or longer. Users are strongly advised to close their positions and/or transfer their assets from Margin Accounts to Spot Accounts before margin trading ceases. Binance will not be responsible for any potential losses. Refer to these FAQs (Binance Portfolio Margin Trading Overview and How Are Liquidations Triggered on Binance Margin) for more information on liquidation details for Cross and Portfolio Margin Accounts.Binance will relist SNDKB on Cross and Portfolio Margin after completing the stock split.
Convert
Binance Convert (including Convert Low-Value Assets) will pause the conversion of SNDKB and all associated pairs at 2026-11-20 00:30 (UTC). Users may choose to convert their low-value SNDKB assets before this time.
Convert will resume support for SNDKB at 2026-11-20 09:00 (UTC).
Buy & Sell Crypto
At 2026-11-19 23:30 (UTC), Buy & Sell Crypto will suspend SNDKB and all associated pairs. Users are advised to complete any pending SNDKB orders before this time.Buy & Sell Crypto will resume support for SNDKB and all associated pairs after the distribution is completed.
