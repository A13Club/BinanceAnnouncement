# Binance Launches Limited-Time Futures Taker Program Promotion (2026-11-10)

This is a general announcement and marketing communication. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.
Fellow Binancians,
As Binance conducts regular reviews of its programs to provide users with the most value and competitive services, Binance will be launching a limited-time promotion for its USDⓈ-M Futures Taker Program, effective from 2026-11-10 06:00 (UTC) until 2027-01-03 23:59 (UTC).
Key Highlights:
During the Promotion Period, the taker fee discounts for Tier 2 and Tier 3 will be increased from 14% to 15% and from 18% to 20%, respectively.Tier 1 remains unchanged at 10%, and all tier qualification thresholds remain unchanged.
Promotion Period: 2026-11-10 06:00 (UTC) to 2027-01-03 23:59 (UTC).
How to Participate:
Users who reach a total Binance Futures trading volume of 100,000,000 USDT equivalent in the last 30 days may submit the application form for the Taker Program at any time, and be assessed for eligibility in the Program's weekly review. The Taker Fee Discount will be effective from the following Tuesday at approximately 07:00 (UTC) after the weekly review.

Terms & Conditions:
