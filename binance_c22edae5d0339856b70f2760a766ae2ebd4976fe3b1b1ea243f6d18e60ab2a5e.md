# Binance Will Support the 0G (0G) BEP20 Contract Swap

This is a general announcement and marketing communication. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.
Fellow Binancians,
Binance will support the 0G (0G) contract swap on BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network.
General
Deposits and WithdrawalsAt 2026-11-30 15:00 (UTC), deposits and withdrawals of the 0G token via the old BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network will be suspended. Please note that the old 0G (BEP20) tokens will no longer be supported for deposits or withdrawals thereafter. Users should ensure they leave sufficient time for the aforementioned token’s deposits to be fully processed prior to this time. Once the contract swap is complete, Binance will open deposits and withdrawals of the new 0G (BEP20) tokens without any further notice.Spot, Margin, Futures trading and Binance Earn services will not be impacted during the contract swap.Binance will handle all technical requirements for users who are involved in this event.
Contract Swap
This contract swap will be conducted at a ratio of 1:1.
