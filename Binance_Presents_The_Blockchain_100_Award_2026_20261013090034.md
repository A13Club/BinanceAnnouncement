# Binance Presents The Blockchain 100 Award 2026

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

The Blockchain 100 Award, presented by Binance in collaboration with global media and ecosystem partners, is an annual award that honors financial content creators who have made a profound impact on the industry through education, information sharing, and inspirational content. Binance invites you to participate and help decide who deserves a place on the list.

How to Nominate Creators:

Anyone can nominate creators through the nomination survey on The Blockchain 100 event page, including self-nominations. You may nominate creators for different award categories. Each nomination can include the same creator for up to one category. There are five award categories in total:

Award CategoryDescriptionsCommunity Influencer of the YearEstablished macro-influencers, evaluated based on how widely their content is shared and discussed across the industry, as well as the length of time they have contributed to the space.Trader of the YearTraders with a verifiable live trading record who openly share their strategies, market views and trading insights, helping the community trade with more knowledge and discipline.Content Creator of the Year - Best Live StreamerContent creators who focus on livestream content and demonstrate consistent output.Content Creator of the Year - Best Longform CreatorContent creators who focus on long-form articles or video content and demonstrate consistent output.Content Creator of the Year - Rising StarUp-and-coming creators who show exceptional promise in producing impactful crypto content across any platform.

Note:

Media and ally partners of The Blockchain 100 can also submit nominations. The nominee’s country or region of residence does not affect eligibility.

Nomination Period: 2026-10-13 09:00 (UTC) to 2026-10-16 15:59 (UTC)

Rules and Eligibility:

The nominated creators must fulfill the basic eligibility criteria and the additional requirement under each award category or sub-category to be shortlisted. 

Basic eligibility criteria:Must be a content creator who primarily produces educational or inspirational content on finance-related topics, including traditional finance and crypto;Must have a public-facing identity with no major controversies;Must have an active account with fewer than five Binance Square violations in since 2026-01-01, and no bans on Binance Square during the event (2026-10-13 09:00 UTC to 2026-10-29 09:00 UTC) or on major social platforms (e.g. X, YouTube).Additional requirements:Community Influencer of the Year (meet all of the following):Follower Count: Must have a minimum of 100,000 genuine followers on at least one major social media platformImpact: Evaluated based on how widely their content is shared and discussed across the industryTenure: Consideration will be given to how long the creator has been contributing to the industryTrader of the Year (meet at least one of the following):A verifiable trading record on Binance or any other platform, including Spot, Futures, on-chain or TradFi.Recognized and ranked in top under Binance’s smart money leaderboard, Binance Square leaderboard, lead trader leaderboard, etc. Content Creator of the YearSubmit the nominated creator’s most representative content piece, relevant to the award category for community voting and evaluation.

Shortlisted candidates will proceed to the community voting stage, and the final winners will be determined based on the voting results.

Selection Criteria:

After nominations close, candidates will be evaluated based on multiple factors, including but not limited to:

The number and diversity of valid nominations (from online nominations or industry recommendations);Quantifiable influence, such as audience size (subscribers, followers);Engagement metrics, including views, likes, and shares;Industry impact, such as driving industry adoption, guiding public opinion, or promoting community development.

Shortlisted candidates will proceed to the community voting stage, and the final winners will be determined based on the voting results.

Community Voting:

Each Binance verified user can cast one vote per category and sub-category per day, with a maximum of five votes daily. Voting counts refresh daily at 00:00 (UTC). Remaining votes can be viewed on the event page. Rankings update daily at 00:00 (UTC) on the event page but are not final. Final results will be confirmed and announced on 2026-10-29 09:00 (UTC).

All votes must comply with the event Terms and Conditions, ineligible or rule-violating votes will be removed after the Voting Period. The final valid vote count will be published after Voting Period ends. After voting, each category will select winners from the top-ranked users on the leaderboard, totaling 100 creators.

Voting Period: 2026-10-19 09:00 (UTC) to 2026-10-28 15:59 (UTC)
