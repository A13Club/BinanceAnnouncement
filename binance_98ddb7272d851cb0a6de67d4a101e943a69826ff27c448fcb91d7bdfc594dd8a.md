# CIS & CEE Exclusive: Binance P2P Launches Merchant Referral Program

This is a general announcement. Products and services mentioned may not be available in your region. Terms and conditions apply. 
Fellow Binancians,
We are launching a long-term program, the "Merchant Referral Program". For each merchant you successfully refer to join Binance P2P, you can receive a token voucher worth 50 USDC. The referred merchant may also receive a token voucher worth 50 or 100 USDC for completing the trading tasks.
Campaign Period: 2026-10-29 23:59 (UTC) to 2026-12-31 23:59 (UTC)
How to Participate:
Step 1. Log in to your binance account and complete identity verification. If you are already verified, proceed to Step 2. 
Step 2. Fill in and submit the referral form.
Step 3. The referred merchant completes onboarding.
Binance merchants must submit an application and pass verification via this link.Merchants and experienced traders from other platforms must submit an application and pass verification via this link.
Step 4. The referred merchant completes the first trade. They post a P2P ad and complete their first P2P trade. After verification, the referrer receives 50 USDC in token voucher.
Step 5. The referred merchant completes the trading tasks. After completing Level 1 or 2, they receive a token voucher worth 50 or 100 USDC.
Trading Tasks for Referred Merchants
Trade only with the eligible fiat currencies: KZT, KGS, AMD, AZN, GEL, TJS, BYN, UAH, MDL. Referred Merchants can receive rewards according to the following requirements:
Level 1: Complete 60 P2P trades with different users, reaching a total volume of at least 20,000 USDT equivalent. Reward: 50 USDC.Level 2: Complete 120 P2P trades with different users, reaching a total volume of at least 40,000 USDT equivalent. Reward: 100 USDC.
Note: Users can receive only one reward. If a user completes both tasks they will receive the highest reward they are eligible for.
Merchant Eligibility Criteria:
The referred merchant must fulfill all of the requirements: 
This campaign is only available to new Binance P2P merchants whose Binance account KYC country / region is Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Tajikistan, Belarus, Ukraine, Moldova.Be an active merchant for at least 10 days from the onboarding date (cumulative, not necessarily consecutive). A day counts as active if it has at least one completed order.Order completion rate must be at least 80% within 30 days of onboarding.
Results Evaluation:
Results are evaluated every two weeks. The referrer receives the reward on the nearest review date after the referred merchant's onboarding and first completed trade are confirmed. The referred merchant receives the reward on the review date after completing Level 1 or 2 tasks.
If a level has not yet been completed, the merchant remains in the program and is checked in the next cycle. Trading tasks must be completed no more than 30 days from the referred merchant's onboarding date. Tasks not completed within this period are not counted toward the program.
