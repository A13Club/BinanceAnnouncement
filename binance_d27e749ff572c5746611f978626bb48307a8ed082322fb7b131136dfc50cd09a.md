# Introducing Sub-Affiliate: Flexible Commission Sharing on Binance

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. 
Fellow Binancians,
We are excited to launch the Binance Sub-Affiliate feature. It allows Affiliates to share a portion of their own commission rate with Sub-Affiliates they invite to their team. Sub-Affiliates may in turn invite further Sub-Affiliates, up to a maximum of 7 levels.   
Key Highlights:
Tiered team structure: Share commissions with your team across up to 7 levels.Up to 50 Sub-Affiliates: Each Top Affiliate may invite up to 50 Sub-Affiliates in total.Commission sharing for every level: Each level can set the commission rate for its direct Sub-Affiliates, subject to the applicable commission rules.Minimum allocation: When creating an invitation, Affiliates must allocate at least 20% (Spot and Futures) of the commission rate to the new Sub-Affiliate, while keeping at least 5% for themselves.Capped total payout: The team’s total payout never exceeds the Top Affiliate's commission rate.
Joining is free. Commissions are earned from genuine trading activity on Binance, not from referrals alone, and inviting additional Sub-Affiliates is entirely optional. The total commission distributed by Binance to any team will never exceed the Affiliate’s own commission rate, and each participant receives their commission directly from Binance.
Commission rates are not guaranteed and may be adjusted or reduced to zero at any time, including where another participant in your team is restricted for compliance reasons. Binance may also recover commissions that have already been paid if they were generated through activity that breached the applicable terms. 
Please refer to the Sub-Affiliate Feature FAQ for more details.
Commission Allocation Rules:
When inviting a Sub-Affiliate, the following rules apply:
The Affiliate must allocate at least 20% of their Spot commission rate and at least 20% of their Futures commission rate to the new Sub-Affiliate.Each participant must retain for themselves at least 5% of the overall commission rate, rather than merely 5% of their own allocated share.
These requirements apply at every level of the Sub-Affiliate network.
Please note that this 5% minimum applies only at setup. It does not guarantee that the Affiliate or Sub-Affiliate will continue to receive at least 5% if the total commission rate is later adjusted, such as due to the Affiliate evaluation downgrade. In such cases, the reduction is applied first to the Affiliate’s retained share, which may fall below 5% or even 0% before the next level’s allocated rate is affected.
Who Can Become a Sub-Affiliate:
Eligible Binance users can become a Sub-Affiliate upon accepting the invitation from an existing Affiliate. To be eligible, the user must:
complete and pass identity verification (KYC) before accepting the invitation;have an activated Binance Futures Account;not be an Affiliate and must not have been an Affiliate previously;not be a Sub-Affiliate under another Affiliate;not be a Co-Inviter; andnot reside in a restricted country or region where the Binance Referral Program and/or the Sub-Affiliate feature is not supported.
For more information please refer to the Sub-Affiliate Feature FAQ and Terms & Conditions.
