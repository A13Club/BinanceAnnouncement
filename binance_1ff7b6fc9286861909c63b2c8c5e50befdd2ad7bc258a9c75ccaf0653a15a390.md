Step 1: Play the Binance Blockchain Week Pixel Mini Game, then take a screenshot of your score. You can play the game as many times as you want to reach the highest score on your screenshot.

Step 3: In your post, write your score and add #BlockchainWeek.
Step 4: Join the Binance Discord Blockchain Week discussion channel to share the best strategies for maximizing your points.
The top 10 users with the highest scores will share a prize pool of 500 USDC in token vouchers.
