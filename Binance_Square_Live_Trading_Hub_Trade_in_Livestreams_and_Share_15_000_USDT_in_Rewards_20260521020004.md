Fellow Binancians,

Binance is excited to announce a promotion to celebrate the launch of Live Trading Hub, a new social trading feature on Binance Square Live. Creators and viewers who participate during the Promotion Period can share in a total prize pool of 15,000 USDT.

Live Trading Hub lets creators share real-time trading data during livestreams — positions, PnL, and trade history — while viewers join in and compete on a live leaderboard. It's social trading, live. 

Promotion Period: 2026-05-21 00:00 (UTC) to 2026-05-30 23:59 (UTC)

How to Participate:

Creators: Livestream access is unlocked automatically once you reach 1,000 Binance Square followers. Start a livestream on Binance Square and enable "Live Trading Hub", you will be automatically entered into the creator promotions.

*If you do not yet meet the follower requirement but already have livestreaming experience or a strong content creation background, you can apply for livestream access through our survey (Approved applicants will be contacted via DM).

Viewers: Join any livestream with Live Trading Hub enabled and trade. No separate registration is required.

Reward Structure: 

For Binance Square Creators: 10,000 USDT in Rewards Up for Grabs!

Creators who host livestreams using the Live Trading Hub feature during the Promotion Period can compete for a share of 10,000 USDT across two leaderboards. To be eligible for rewards, creators must host at least three Valid Sessions during the promotion.

Promotion A: Popular Streamer Leaderboard (5,000 USDT)

Creators will be ranked based on their cumulative number of Valid Participants across all their Valid Sessions during the promotion period.

Valid Trade: Only trades and PnL from new positions opened after a user joins a Live Trading Hub session will be counted. Pre-existing positions and their PnL are excluded from this campaign. Trading volume from stablecoin-to-stablecoin pairs will not be counted.Valid Participant: A viewer who joins a Live Trading Hub session and completes a cumulative trading volume of at least 100 USDT in Valid Trades during that session.Valid Session: A single Live Trading Hub session within a livestream that runs for a continuous duration of at least 30 minutes and has at least three Valid Participants.Example: Creator A hosted 4 Live Trading Hub sessions during the Promotion Period, of which 3 were Valid Sessions with 15, 22, and 18 Valid Participants respectively. Creator A's cumulative Valid Participants count = 15 + 22 + 18 = 55, and they will be ranked based on this total.

Promotion B: Skilled Streamer Leaderboard (5,000 USDT Prize Pool)

Creators will be ranked by their single best session's Realized PnL during the Promotion Period. Only sessions in New Positions mode will be counted (including Realized PnL from new positions opened by both the streamer and participants).

For Viewers: New TradFi Futures Trader Bonus (5,000 USDT)

The first 1,666 users who complete all of the following will each receive a 3 USDT Futures Bonus Voucher:

1.     Join any Live Trading Hub session during the Promotion Period.

2.     Make their first-ever TradFi USDⓈ-M Futures trade within a Live Trading Hub session.

3.     Achieve ≥ 100 USDT in TradFi USDⓈ-M Futures trading volume within the session.

Rewards will be distributed on a first-come, first-served basis.
