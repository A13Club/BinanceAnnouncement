Fellow Binancians,
Binance is excited to announce that Binance Pay now enables eligible users in South Africa to pay for in-store purchases at participating Pick n Pay stores using crypto via QR code. 
To celebrate, eligible users can earn 20% cashback in USDC on their first qualifying Pick n Pay in-store purchase made using Binance Pay during the Promotion Period, capped at 5 USDC per eligible user on a first-come, first-served basis.
Pay with crypto. Get crypto back. Shop in store at Pick n Pay.

20% cashback in USDCMinimum qualifying spend of R90 (in one successful transaction)Maximum reward of 5 USDC per eligible userOne reward per eligible userTotal reward pool of 5,000 USDCRewards available on a first-come, first-served basis
Promotion Period (South Africa Standard Time):
The Promotion begins on 2026-11-16 and ends on 2027-01-16, or when the total reward pool of 5,000 USDC has been fully allocated, whichever comes first.The Promotion may end early once the reward pool is depleted. No further transactions will qualify for cashback after the available reward allocation has been exhausted.
How to Participate:
Shop in store at participating Pick n Pay stores.At the till, ask to pay using QR code or Scan to Pay.The card machine will display a payment QR code and the ZAR amount.Open the Binance App and go to Binance Pay, then scan the QR code.Review the payment details, select an eligible crypto balance, and confirm.Complete your purchase. The merchant receives ZAR, and you pay in crypto.If your transaction qualifies, Binance will credit the cashback reward in USDC to your Binance account.
Reward Calculation and Caps:
Cashback is calculated as 20% of the final eligible purchase value in ZAR, and credited in USDC.Maximum reward: 5 USDC per eligible user.Minimum qualifying spend: R90 in one successful transaction.Based on the indicative rate above, the 5 USDC cap is reached at approximately R450 spend, and the minimum qualifying spend of R90 corresponds to approximately 1 USDC cashback.
Eligibility
Available to eligible users in South Africa only.You must complete identity verification (KYC) to receive a reward.Only the user’s first eligible in-store payment successfully processed and settled through Binance Pay via the QR flow at a participating Pick n Pay store during the Promotion period will qualify.Transactions must pass applicable Binance and MoneyBadger risk checks.Pending, failed, canceled, refunded, reversed, disputed, duplicated, manipulated, abusive, or fraudulent transactions will not qualify.
