# Introducing Binance AI Pro

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. Terms and conditions apply. 

Fellow Binancians,

Binance is excited to announce the official launch of Binance AI Pro, a finance-focused AI agent that turns your trading ideas into tested, automated strategies through a simple conversation, the parameters of which are fully decided by you. From 2026-10-19, Binance AI Pro will roll out to all users globally  on the website and the Binance app.

Binance AI Pro is part of Binance Intelligence, Binance’s suite of AI products. The suite also includes Binance Agent OS and Binance AI.

Key Features and Advantages:

Describe your idea in plain words: Tell Binance AI Pro what you want to do, the same way you would explain it to a friend. It helps fill in the gaps, improves your strategy and turns it into a visual workflow for you to review. No coding or separate app download is required on your end.See your strategy as a workflow: On desktop, you can view the chat and your strategy workflow side by side. Click any step within the workflow to ask questions, make changes, add new elements or remove existing ones. On the Binance app, you can build and refine strategies directly through chat.Test before you trade: Try your strategy with “paper trading”, which uses hypothetical funds, before you use real funds. Adjust it until it works the way you want.Built for finance, native to Binance: Binance AI Pro uses Binance’s market data and research, along with selected third-party sources. Everything you need to optimise your strategy is already available in one place.Choose your AI model: Both Standard Plan users and Premium Plan users will have access to DeepSeek. Premium Plan users can also use leading frontier models such as ChatGPT Sol, ChatGPT Astra and Claude Opus.

How to Get Started:

Binance AI Pro is available to all Binance users without any separate sign-up. Simply log into your Binance account, and follow these steps:

On the App:

Tap the Binance AI icon on the homepage.Tap the [Get more with AI Pro] banner to open Binance AI Pro.Describe your trading idea in the chat.Review your strategy and test it with paper trading.Approve the strategy, then transfer funds to its dedicated sub-account to start live trading.

On the Web:

Open Binance AI Pro from [AI] in the top navigation bar, or go directly to ai.binance.com.Follow the same steps 3 to 5 above.

For Binance AI Pro (Beta) Users:

Your Beta stays active: Binance AI Pro (Beta) will continue to run as usual, and your existing subscription stays active. No action is needed.Use both products: Binance AI Pro does not replace the Beta. You can use both at the same time.Optional migration: If you’d like, you can move some of your Beta data to the new Binance AI Pro, including your chat history and strategies. 

To start, open Binance AI Pro (Beta) and find the migration option there. For more details, please refer to this FAQ.
