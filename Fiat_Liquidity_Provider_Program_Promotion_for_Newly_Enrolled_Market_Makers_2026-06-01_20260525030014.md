# Fiat Liquidity Provider Program Promotion for Newly Enrolled Market Makers (2026-06-01)

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

Binance is pleased to introduce a limited-time promotion for the Fiat Liquidity Provider Program. Newly enrolled fiat market makers who meet the minimum qualification requirement in eligible fiat markets will receive the highest available maker fee rebate rate for those markets during the Promotion Period.

Promotion Period: 2026-06-01 00:00 (UTC) to 2026-08-30 23:59 (UTC)

Eligible Users: Available to qualified market makers who enroll in the Binance Fiat Liquidity Provider Program after 2026-05-11 00:00 (UTC). 

Key Updates

Participants in the eligible markets will automatically be upgraded to the highest available rebate rate for the duration of the Promotion Period.Participants will need to be qualified in the eligible markets (achieve Maker Volume Percentage of 0.5%) in order to receive any rebates. Eligible markets include TRY, EUR, BRL, JPY, and USD.The qualification review of promotional rebates will be effective starting from 2026-06-01 00:00 (UTC). Liquidity providers will be reviewed weekly based on the new performance review mechanism.Maker fee rebates will be updated on a weekly basis, from 2026-06-09 00:00 (UTC). Maker Fee Rebates will be distributed to liquidity providers based on their previous week’s Spot trading performance across the selected fiat markets.Existing Fiat Liquidity Provider Program participants enrolled before 2026-05-11 00:00 (UTC) are not eligible.

Program Incentives (Applicable to Participants Qualifying for at Least One of the Eligible Fiat Markets)

Qualified Fiat Markets: Maker rebates and standard VIP taker fees on all pairs across the eligible fiat markets.Unqualified Fiat Markets: Zero maker fees and standard VIP taker fees on all fiat pairs across these fiat markets.

How to Participate

Users whose 30-day trading volume exceeds 20,000,000 USDT equivalent on Binance Spot and Margin or other platforms, and exhibit quality liquidity providing strategies, are invited to join the Fiat Liquidity Provider Program. To apply for a trial period, please provide proof of your trading volume to mmprogram@binance.com, or contact your sales or key account coverage team.
