# Binance Stock Trading Introduces Recurring Buy Feature

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

Binance is pleased to introduce Stock Recurring Buy, a new feature that allows eligible users to schedule recurring buys of selected stocks and ETFs on a fixed schedule without placing manual orders each time.

With Stock Recurring Buy, users can build recurring plans more conveniently and maintain a regular purchase routine. Each Recurring Buy plan supports either a single stock or multiple stocks within one plan.

Stock Recurring Buy will support the following 100 stocks and ETFs at 2026-09-10 10:00 (UTC):

AAPL, ABBV, ABT, ADBE, AMAT, AMD, AMZN, ARKK, ARM, ASML, AVGO, BABA, BAC, BLK, BRK-B, BX, C, COIN, COST, CRM, CVX, DHR, DIA, DIS, DUOL, GLD, GOOGL, GS, HD, HOOD, IEFA, INTC, INTU, ISRG, IWM, JEPI, JEPQ, JNJ, JPM, KLAC, KO, LLY, LRCX, MA, MCD, META, MRK, MS, MSFT, MU, NFLX, NKE, NOW, NVDA, ORCL, PANW, PEP, PFE, PG, PLTR, PYPL, QCOM, QQQ, RBLX, RDDT, SBUX, SCHD, SCHW, SLV, SMCI, SMH, SNOW, SOFI, SOXL, SOXX, SPY, SQQQ, TLT, TQQQ, TSLA, TSM, TXN, UNH, V, VEA, VOO, VTI, VUG, VWO, WFC, WMT, XLB, XLE, XLF, XLI, XLK, XLU, XLV, XLY and XOM.
