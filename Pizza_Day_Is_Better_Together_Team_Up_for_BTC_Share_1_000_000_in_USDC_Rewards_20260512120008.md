How to Participate

Step 1: Visit the Activity Page.

Step 2: Build or Join a Team

Build a team as leader: Choose to build a 3, 5, or 10-person team as a leader and invite others via your team/referral invitation link/code during the task period.During the Promotion Period, each user can create and complete up to 10 teams as a team leader. *A completed team refers to a team where BTC conversion is executed for all members.A user (leader) can only create a new team once their current team has been completed. 

Join a team as a member: Join a team with an invitation link from a friend or directly input the team/referral invitation link. Each user can only join a team as a member once during the Promotion Period. The member may choose to leave the team up to 6 hours before the Task Period ends. Once the Task Period enters the final 6 hours, members will no longer be able to leave the team.

Step 3: Lock Funds

Each participant (both leader and members) are required to click the [lock] button on the activity page to lock 100 USDC to confirm their spot in the team.

Step 4: BTC Conversion

Once the team is full and all members have successfully locked their 100 USDC, the system will automatically convert the leader and each member's locked USDC into BTC in real-time market price.

Step 5: Receive Rewards

After the successful conversion, the leader and all team members will receive USDC rewards directly in their Spot accounts within 48 hours.

Please note: 

All participants are subject to periodic risk assessment. Failure to pass the risk review may result in removal from the team or ineligibility for rewards. Please refer to the Terms and Conditions for more information.

New User Exclusive Benefit

New users who register either using the referral code “PIZZADAY26”, or via a participant’s team/referral invitation link/code can enjoy an exclusive benefit during the Promotion Period — convert BTC individually without forming a team and receive 3 USDC.

Eligibility Condition

The new user benefit will be granted only if the team is not successfully completed, and the USDC to BTC conversion (Step 4) is not executed.

If the team is not completed, eligible new users will be granted benefit to the individual BTC conversion benefit. New users can complete the required steps (e.g., locking funds) on the activity page and proceed with the BTC conversion to receive the 3 USDC reward.If the team is successfully completed and BTC conversion is executed, the new user will receive the standard team reward instead, and the new user benefit will not apply.
