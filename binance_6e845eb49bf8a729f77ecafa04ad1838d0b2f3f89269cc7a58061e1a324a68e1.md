# Binance Futures Will End Last Price Protected Period on USDⓈ-Margined SNDKUSDT Perpetual Contract (2026-11-20)

This is a general Binance Exchange Notice. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region
Fellow Binancians,
Binance Futures will end Last Price Protected  (“LPP”) period on USDⓈ-Margined SNDKUSDT Perpetual Contract and the Mark Price will transition gradually from LPP mechanism to standard Mark Price calculation at 2026-11-20 10:00 (UTC). The transition period may take up to 3 hours, subject to price volatility and availability of stable index price. The transition will be applied gradually by using mark price smoothing process to minimise sudden price movements and to better ensure price stability during the transition. Trading function is not affected during the transition period. Open orders and positions will not be cancelled. 
About Mark Price:
Mark Price After the LPP Period Ends:
When the LPP period of the aforementioned contract ends, the Mark Price will be calculated using the following formula:Mark Price = Median (Price 1, Price 2, Contract Price)
Please refer to the Mark Price and Price Index in USDⓈ-Margined Futures for Price 1 and Price 2 formulas.
