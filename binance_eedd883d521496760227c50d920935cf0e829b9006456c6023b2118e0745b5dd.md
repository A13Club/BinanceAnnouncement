# Binance to Adjust Leverage on Select Isolated Margin Trading Pairs

This is a general announcement and marketing communication. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. 
Fellow Binancians,
To strengthen risk management and support a healthier trading environment, Binance will lower the maximum leverage for the following Isolated Margin trading pairs from 10x to 5x, effective 2026-11-10 08:00 (UTC).
Following this adjustment, the Margin liquidation risk ratios and other leverage parameters for these pairs will change accordingly. Users are strongly advised to closely monitor their positions and transfer additional assets into their Isolated Margin accounts or repay outstanding debt in advance to reduce liquidation risk.
