# Binance Wallet to Discontinue Support for Blast

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.
Fellow Binancians, 
Binance Wallet will discontinue support for the Blast chain effective from 2026-11-30 10:00 (UTC). After this date, users will no longer be able to view, send, or receive Blast assets within Binance Wallet.
This change only affects the display and management of these assets within Binance Wallet, users can still claim their assets on Blast official site.
How to Claim Your Blast Assets
Users are strongly advised to manage or transfer their Blast assets before 2026-11-30 10:00 (UTC). For detailed instructions on transferring Blast assets, please refer to Blast’s official site.After support is discontinued on Binance Wallet, users can continue to claim their Blast assets as long as Blast official site for Blast Fund Recovery is functioning. 
Please Note:
Following the shutdown of the Blast chain, please note that assets shown on your Binance Wallet may not reflect the correct balance. Please refer to Blast’s official site for more information.This update does not affect other blockchains supported by Binance Wallet. 
There may be discrepancies between this original content in English and any translated versions. Please refer to the original English version for the most accurate information, in case any discrepancies arise.
