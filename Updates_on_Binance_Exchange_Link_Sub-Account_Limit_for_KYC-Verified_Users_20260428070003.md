# Updates on Binance Exchange Link Sub-Account Limit for KYC-Verified Users

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians, 

Binance will implement an update to Binance Exchange Link (also known as Crypto-as-a-Service: Basic/Advanced), to set the sub-account limit for KYC-verified linkages per Link client's end user. This change aligns our platform-wide standards across our products and services to enhance compliance and security measures for all users. 

Key Changes

Effective 2026-04-29 00:00 (UTC), the maximum number of sub-accounts per KYC-verified user per Binance Exchange Link Client will be set to 5. 

What Remains Unchanged: 

Exchange Link clients may continue to request higher overall sub-account limits for their accounts. Exchange Link clients may continue to create and manage sub-accounts as needed to onboard new users.Existing sub-accounts and KYC linkages created before the effective date and time will remain unaffected. However, if the KYC-to-sub-account limit has already been reached, no new sub-accounts may be linked to a verified KYC profile.

For more information, please contact your dedicated account manager or contact the Binance Link Team.
