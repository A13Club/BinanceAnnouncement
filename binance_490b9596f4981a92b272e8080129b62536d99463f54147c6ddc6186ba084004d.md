Fellow Binancians,
Binance Earn is running a limited-time BTC Yield (BTCY) promotion, offering additional rewards for both new net subscriptions and existing holdings.
Promotion Period: 2026-11-17 03:00 (UTC) to 2026-12-16 23:59 (UTC)
During the Promotion Period, eligible VIP 1 to VIP 9 users may receive an additional APR Boost of up to 2% on new net subscriptions and 0.5% on existing holdings. Eligible Regular users may receive up to 1% on new net subscriptions and 0.25% on existing holdings.

How to Participate
New net subscriptions: Users who make new net BTCY subscriptions on or after 2026-11-17 00:00 (UTC) will be eligible for additional APR Boost rewards for new holdings during the Promotion Period. 
Existing holdings: Users who hold BTCY at the snapshot taken on 2026-11-17 00:00 (UTC) will be automatically enrolled. The BTCY balance recorded will be treated as the user’s existing holdings, and may receive the APR Boost.  
Each user’s holdings will be split daily into existing and new holdings, as follows:
Daily Existing Holdings = The lower of (i) the user’s BTCY holdings on that day and (ii) the user’s snapshot balance.Daily New Holdings = The user’s BTCY holdings on that day minus the user’s snapshot balance. If the value is negative, it will be zero.
Note: Redeeming existing BTCY holdings and subsequently re-subscribing will not be considered new net subscriptions and will not qualify for additional rewards during the Promotion Period.

Notes:
The total reward pool is equivalent to 70,000 USD in BTCY. Rewards are limited and available on a first-come, first-served basis until the reward pool is depleted. BTCY exchange rate is calculated based on the conversion rate announced the day the campaign ends.The additional APR applies to eligible BTCY holdings from 0.01 BTCY to 20 BTCY. Users may receive APR Boost on up to 20 BTCY of new holdings and up to 20 BTCY of existing holdings.For the portion exceeding 20 BTCY in either category, the additional APR does not apply. Only the native yield generated under the standard BTC Yield product rules will be earned.Account Aggregation: Holdings of the master account and its sub-accounts will be aggregated for calculation and are subject to a single reward cap; sub-accounts are not eligible for a separate allocation.The APR boost for each holdings tier applies to the entire holdings, not just the portion within that tier. For example, a VIP user with Daily Average New Holdings of 15 BTCY receives a 2% APR Boost on all 15 BTCY. The tier does not affect the APR Boost for existing holdings.
VIP Reward Eligibility
To qualify for the additional 2% APR Boost Reward for new subscriptions or 0.5% for existing subscriptions, VIP users must continuously maintain their Binance VIP status at any tier, throughout all 30 Reward Calculation Days during the Promotion Period. If the user becomes a VIP after the Promotion has started or loses VIP status during the Promotion Period, they will not be eligible.
Binance will record each user’s VIP status daily at 00:00 (UTC). If a user does not meet the VIP eligibility requirement on any Reward Calculation Day, all rewards for the Promotion will be calculated using the Regular user APR Boost rate.
VIP status and VIP tier will be determined based on Binance’s system records.
Reward Calculation:
Binance will record each user’s confirmed BTCY holdings daily at 00:00 (UTC) during the Promotion Period.
Daily eligible holdings will be calculated as follows:
If a user’s BTCY holdings are below 0.01 BTCY on a given day, the eligible holdings for that day will be zero.If a user’s BTCY holdings are equal to or greater than 0.01 BTCY, the actual holdings will be counted, subject to a maximum of 20 BTCY per day.
The average eligible holdings during the Promotion Period will be calculated as follows:
Daily Average Eligible Holdings = Sum of Daily Eligible Holdings for All 30 Reward Calculation Days / 30
VIP User APR Boost Reward for new net subscriptions: Reward = (Daily Average Eligible Holdings × APR × 30) / 365Regular User APR Boost Reward for new net subscriptions: Reward = (Daily Average Eligible Holdings × APR × 30) / 365VIP User APR Boost Reward for existing holdings: Reward = (Daily Average Eligible Holdings × 0.50% × 30) / 365Regular User APR Boost Reward for existing holdings: Reward = (Daily Average Eligible Holdings × 0.25% × 30) / 365.
