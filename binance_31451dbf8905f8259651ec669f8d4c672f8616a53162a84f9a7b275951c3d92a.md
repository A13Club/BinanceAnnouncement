# Important Updates on Dividend Adjustment Process of SAMSUNGUSDT TradFi Perpetual Contract (2026-11-06)

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.
Fellow Binancians,
Binance Futures will reconcile 1,001 won per share which equivalent to -0.037336% as dividend adjustment amount for the USDⓈ-Margined SAMSUNGUSDT Perpetual Contract at 2026-11-06 06:00 (UTC). The dividend adjustment process will take approximately 30 minutes to complete. 
The dividend adjustment amount applies to users who have held net positions in the USDⓈ-Margined SAMSUNGUSDT Perpetual Contract on the ex-dividend date, 2026-09-28 23:00 (UTC). Eligible users may check the dividend adjustment amount by navigating to “Transaction History” and select the “Special Funding Fee” type. Notes:
The dividend adjustment amount will be credited or deducted from users’ Cross Margin Futures Accounts. Users are advised to assess the dividend adjustment amount and manage their positions accordingly before the reconciliation takes place.The funding interval will remain unchanged and the contract will not be placed into reduce-only mode before the reconciliation takes place.
