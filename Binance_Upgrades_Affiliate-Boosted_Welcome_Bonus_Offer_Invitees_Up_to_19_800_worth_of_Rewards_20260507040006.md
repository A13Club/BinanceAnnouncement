# Binance Upgrades Affiliate-Boosted Welcome Bonus: Offer Invitees Up to $19,800 worth of Rewards!

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. Terms and conditions apply. 

Fellow Binancians,

Binance is excited to announce a major upgrade to its Affiliate Program with the new Affiliate-Boosted Welcome Bonus. This enhancement features a revamped task structure and significantly increased rewards, allowing Affiliates to provide more attractive incentives to their invitees.

Effective from 2026-05-07 04:00 (UTC), Affiliates who have the previous Affiliate Welcome Bonus will be automatically migrated to the new Affiliate-Boosted Welcome Bonus. No additional application is required.

Key Program Upgrades:

Enhanced Reward Pool: New users can now unlock welcome bonus packages worth up to $19,800, a substantial increase from the previous $600.Structured and Customized Tiered Tasks: The program introduces a customized and tiered task system for each new invited user, informing new users on the actions required to unlock their rewards.Diverse Reward Vouchers: Rewards are distributed as customized vouchers, which may include Trading Fee Rebate Vouchers, Earn Trial Fund Vouchers, and more. The specific voucher type may vary by user and task.

How to Participate:

For Affiliates: All Binance Affiliates are eligible to apply for the Affiliate-Boosted Welcome Bonus. Simply navigate to the Affiliate Pro page and click [Apply Now] to enroll. Once approved, you can invite new users through your referral pro links and start growing your network with even greater incentives.

Apply Now

Note: Each Affiliate is subject to a 30-day allocation cap. Only the first 1,000 eligible new users who sign up within each cycle will be assigned the tiered tasks and rewards. Once the cap is reached, newly invited users will not receive Phase 2 welcome bonus tasks or rewards until the next cycle begins.

For New Users:

To enjoy the enhanced welcome bonuses, new users must sign up through the referral pro link of an Affiliate who has successfully joined the program. Welcome Bonus tasks and rewards will be released in two phases:

Phase 1: Tasks with rewards of up to $100 in value will be assigned upon registration.Phase 2: Additional tasks with rewards of up to $19,700 in value will be assigned within 2 hours after account verification (KYC) is completed. If the user doesn’t complete KYC within 30 days after registration, no additional tasks with rewards will be assigned.

Upon completing the tasks within the specified period, new users will receive the corresponding rewards in their Rewards Hub.

For more information, please refer to Affiliate-Boosted Welcome Bonus.
