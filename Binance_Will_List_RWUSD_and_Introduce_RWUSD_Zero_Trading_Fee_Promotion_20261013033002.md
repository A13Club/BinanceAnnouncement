About RWUSD

RWUSD is a yield product with flexible-term subscriptions and daily APR rewards. Rewards come from Binance’s ecosystem income and can be supported by innovative real-world assets like US Treasury Bills. Designed for relatively stable returns, RWUSD aims to perform well in both rising and falling markets. It represents your subscribed assets plus accrued rewards, pegged 1:1 to the stablecoin you use. Upon redemption, RWUSD will be converted to USDC.

Note: RWUSD currently does not support deposit or withdrawal functionality.
