# Binance Wallet Launches a “Double Waiver” Promotion for ONDO Tokenized Securities: Zero Service Fees & Zero On-Chain Gas Fees

This is a general announcement. Products and services mentioned here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

Binance Wallet will launch a Double Fee Waiver Promotion for trading ONDO tokenized securities. During the Promotion Period, eligible users who trade listed ONDO tokenized securities on the BSC network through Binance Wallet (Keyless) will enjoy both zero service fees and zero on-chain gas fees for buy and sell transactions.

Promotion Period: 2026-05-18 12:00 (UTC) to 2026-06-18 12:00 (UTC)

Promotion Details:

During the Promotion Period, users who trade supported ONDO tokenized U.S. stock securities on the BSC network within Binance Wallet (Keyless) can enjoy the following two fee waivers at the same time:

Zero service fees: During the Promotion Period, all ONDO tokenized U.S. stock securities traded within Binance Wallet (Keyless) will be exempt from service fees. Any subsequent changes will be separately announced.Zero on-chain gas fees: Users will not be required to bear the network gas fees incurred from eligible on-chain transactions.

The Promotion covers all ONDO tokenized U.S. stock securities currently listed in Binance Wallet, including but not limited to: AAPLon (Apple), TSLAon (Tesla), NVDAon (Nvidia), AMZNon (Amazon), QQQon (QQQ), and GLDon (Gold ETF), as well as other ONDO tokenized U.S. stock tokens newly supported by Binance Wallet during the Promotion Period.

Eligible transaction types include:

Buy transactions;Sell transactions;Market orders;Limit orders;On-chain transactions of ONDO tokenized U.S. stock securities on the BSC network supported by Binance Wallet (Keyless).

Platform Scope of Fee Waivers:

The two fee waivers under this Promotion apply as follows:

Zero service fees: Available on the Binance Wallet (Keyless) mobile app (iOS / Android / HarmonyOS) and Binance Wallet (Web). Not available on the Binance Wallet Extension.Zero on-chain gas fees: Available only on the Binance Wallet (Keyless) mobile app (iOS / Android / HarmonyOS). Not available on the Binance Wallet (Web) or the Binance Wallet Extension.

Support for additional entry points may be added later, subject to separate official Binance announcements.

During the Promotion Period, there is no limit on the number of transactions or trading frequency. Any eligible ONDO tokenized U.S. stock securities on-chain transactions completed within Binance Wallet (Keyless) will simultaneously enjoy zero service fees and zero on-chain gas fees.

Eligibility:

This Promotion is only available to Binance Wallet (Keyless) users.Users are not required to click [Join] or complete any additional registration.The transaction activity must take place within Binance Wallet (Keyless).Transactions completed via third-party dApps, external wallets, other trading platforms, or non-Binance Wallet (Keyless) entry points are not eligible for this Promotion.The only network supported for ONDO tokenized U.S. stock securities under this Promotion is the BSC network.

How to Participate:

Open the Binance App, select Wallet and enter Binance Wallet, then go to [Home] > [Securities]. You may directly search for Ondo-related tokenized securities in the search bar within the Wallet.Select any listed ONDO tokenized U.S. stock security.Complete a buy or sell transaction within Binance Wallet (Keyless).Eligible on-chain transactions will automatically enjoy the applicable fee waivers (zero service fees and/or zero gas fees) based on the entry point used, as set out above

under "Platform Scope of Fee Waivers". (The trading order page will display the applicable service fee and gas fee as free.)

Note: Please ensure your Binance App is updated to the latest version.
