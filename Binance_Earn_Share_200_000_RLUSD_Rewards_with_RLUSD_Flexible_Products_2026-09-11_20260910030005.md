Campaign Points Calculation:

A user’s final score is calculated using the formula below:

Total Campaign Points = Task Points * Promotion Period Multiplier + Holding Snapshot Points

Task Points will be calculated by tasks competition, such as subscribing to RLUSD Earn products. Points will be deducted for withdrawing or redeeming assets according to the table below. For details, refer to the below formula and the eligibility task table below.Task Points = (USD Value of Subscription * Points) - (USD Value of Redemption * Points)During the Promotion Period, newly subscribed Flexible Earn assets that are pledged as collateral in Flexible Loan will be included in points calculation as normal.Promotion Period Multiplier: A multiplier determined by how many days remain until the last day of the Promotion Period at the time of subscription.The multiplier starts at 14x on Day 1 and decreases by 1 each day. The earlier a user subscribes during the Promotion Period, the higher the multiplier will be.The multiplier also applies to point deductions from withdrawal/redemption actions, based on the day the action occurs.Please note that the multiplier only applies to the calculation of Campaign Points, and does not affect the actual value of your investment position. Holding Snapshot Points: A one-time bonus points to rewards RLUSD Earn users.Holding Snapshot Points = Total USD value of RLUSD Flexible Product holdings * 30, snapshot taken at 2026-09-08 23:59:59 (UTC).
