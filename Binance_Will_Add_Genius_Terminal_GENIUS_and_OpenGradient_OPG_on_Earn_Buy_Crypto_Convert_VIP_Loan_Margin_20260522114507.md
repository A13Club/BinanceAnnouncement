# Binance Will Add Genius Terminal (GENIUS) and OpenGradient (OPG) on Earn, Buy Crypto, Convert, VIP Loan & Margin

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. 

Fellow Binancians,

Binance is excited to announce that Genius Terminal (GENIUS) and OpenGradient (OPG) will be added to Binance Simple Earn, "Buy Crypto", Binance Convert, Binance Margin and VIP Loan at the respective dates and timings listed below.

Earn

GENIUS Flexible Products and OPG Flexible Products will be listed on Binance Simple Earn at 2026-05-22 12:00 (UTC) and will be available for subscription.

Buy & Sell Crypto

Users can buy GENIUS and OPG with VISA, MasterCard, Google Pay, Apple Pay or buy and sell GENIUS and OPG with their account balances on the “Buy Crypto” page, available within one hour of GENIUS and OPG being listed on Binance Spot. 

Convert

Users will be able to start trading GENIUS and OPG against BTC, USDT, and any other tokens on Binance Convert at zero fees within one hour of GENIUS and OPG being listed on Binance Spot. 

VIP Loan

GENIUS and OPG will be listed as a borrowable coin on VIP Loan within one hour of GENIUS and OPG being listed on Binance Spot. 

Margin

Cross Margin & Isolated Margin

Binance Margin will add GENIUS and OPG as new borrowable assets on Cross and Isolated Margin, as well as the GENIUS/USDT,  GENIUS/USDC,  OPG/USDT and OPG/USDC pairs on Cross and Isolated Margin at 2026-05-22 12:00 (UTC).

Portfolio Margin

Binance Margin will add GENIUS and OPG as new borrowable assets on Portfolio Margin, as well as the  GENIUS/USDT,  GENIUS/USDC,  OPG/USDT and OPG/USDC pairs on Portfolio Margin at 2026-05-22 12:00 (UTC).

Notes: 

Please note that newly listed tokens tend to be volatile; users are encouraged to adopt stringent risk management strategies when trading such tokens.

Please refer to Margin Data for a list of the most updated marginable assets and further information on specific limits, collateral ratio and rates.
