# Introducing Binance Square Leaderboards: Discover Top Traders and Share Your Achievements

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. Terms and conditions apply. 
Fellow Binancians,
Binance is excited to introduce Square Leaderboards, a new feature built directly into Binance Square. Square Leaderboards bring trading rankings together in one place, making it easier to discover traders, track their performance over time, learn more about top performers and explore shared trades..With the new sharing feature, you can also turn your own leaderboard records into personalized posters.
Key Features
Explore trading rankings: Browse Shared Trades for Futures trade-sharing posts, Smart money for top trader signals, and Copy Trading for lead traders' results.Share your current ranking: Generate a personalized poster featuring your current rank on any leaderboard.Showcase recent highlights: Combine your best rankings across leaderboards from the past 7 or 30 days into a single achievement poster.
How to Get Started
Explore the Leaderboards
Binance Square Navigation: Open Binance Leaderboard from the top navigation bar.
Smart Money: Follow the signals top traders trade.Copy Trading: Helps you to make more informed decisions in picking lead traders.Shared Trades: View which posted trades are performing best.
Share Your Current Ranking
Open a leaderboard where you are currently ranked and tap your ranking card at the bottom of the page.Generate your personalized poster, then save or share it.
Share Your Recent Highlights
Tap the ranking record icon at the top right of Binance Leaderboard.Select 7D or 30D to view your best rankings across leaderboards during that period. Tap [Share] to generate your achievement poster, then save or share it.
Explore Binance Leaderboard, discover traders who interest you, and share your own leaderboard moment now!
