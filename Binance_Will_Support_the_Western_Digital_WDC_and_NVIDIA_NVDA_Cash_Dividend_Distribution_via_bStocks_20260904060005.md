# Binance Will Support the Western Digital (WDC) and NVIDIA (NVDA) Cash Dividend Distribution via bStocks

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. bStocks are offered through an Approved Prospectus in the ADGM and are not offered in any other jurisdiction. 

bStocks are Tokenized securities issued by BTech Holdings NVDA Limited, a Binance group affiliate. bStocks are classified as Certificates representing certain Financial Instruments (paragraph 92, Schedule 1 to FSMR). bStocks represent an interest in underlying securities held by the Issuer and are not direct ownership of the underlying Shares. bStocks do not allow holders to directly own a share or stock in the underlying listed company.

Fellow Binancians,

Binance will support the Western Digital (WDC) and NVIDIA (NVDA) cash dividend distribution via bStocks for WDCB and NVDAB holders. Net amount of the cash dividend will be reinvested after applicable withholding taxes, fees, costs, and other deductions, into additional units or fractions of the same underlying security. Eligible users will receive dividends in the form of WDCB and NVDAB bStocks respectively.

On-chain holders of WDCB and NVDAB will receive the bStock dividends through a multiplier adjustment.

Eligibility

Users with WDCB holdings on the record date snapshot at 2026-09-08 00:00 (UTC) will be eligible for the dividend distribution.Users with NVDAB holdings on the record date snapshot at 2026-09-10 00:00 (UTC) will be eligible for the dividend distribution.

General

Trading of WDCB/USDT and NVDAB/USDT will not be affected.WDCB:At 2026-09-04 23:30 (UTC), the token conversion service for 1:1 swaps between WDC stock and WDCB bStocks will be suspended.At 2026-09-07 23:30 (UTC), deposits and withdrawals of WDCB tokens will be suspended. Users should ensure they leave sufficient time for their WDCB token deposits to be fully processed prior to this time. NVDAB:At 2026-09-09 23:30 (UTC), the token conversion service for 1:1 swaps between NVDA stock and NVDAB bStocks will be suspended.At 2026-09-09 23:30 (UTC), deposits and withdrawals of NVDAB will be suspended. Users should ensure they leave sufficient time for their NVDAB deposits to be fully processed prior to this time. Deposits, withdrawals, and token conversion service for WDCB and NVDAB will resume when the distribution is completed. No further announcement will be posted. Users can also subscribe to the recovery notification where available.Dividends earned from assets held in other accounts will be credited to Spot Account, including but not limited to Savings Account, Margin Account, etc.Binance will handle all technical requirements for users who are involved in this event.Reinvestment and distribution of dividends is after deduction of withholding tax. Users may refer to the announcements from the companies for more information.For more information about bStocks dividend distribution, please refer to the FAQ.

Convert

Binance Convert will pause the conversion of WDCB and all associated pairs at 2026-09-07 23:30 (UTC).Binance Convert will pause the conversion of NVDAB and all associated pairs at 2026-09-09 23:30 (UTC).
