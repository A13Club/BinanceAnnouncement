Fellow Binancians,
Binance Blockchain Week Bangkok is here, and the celebration extends to our global community - whether you're on the ground in Bangkok or joining from anywhere in the world. 
Two new challenges are live: show us your Blockchain Week moment, and honor the Community Heroes who make Binance what it is. 
Complete one or both challenges for a chance to share a total prize pool of 1,000 USDC in token vouchers.

How to Participate:
Challenge 1: Share Your Binance Blockchain Week Experience
Step 1: Take a real picture at the event, or create an AI-generated photo of yourself at the Binance Blockchain Week experience.
Step 2: Make a post on X with the picture and the 3 most memorable quotes from the event.
Step 3: Add the hashtag #BinanceBlockchainWeek to your post.
Step 4: Share your post in this Binance Survey.
Binance will select the best 25 posts at its sole discretion. Winners of these 25 posts will share a prize pool of 500 USDC in token vouchers.
Challenge 2: Find Your Binance Community Hero
Step 1: Find a Binance Community Hero at the event and take a picture with him. You can find them in the activities areas with a special T-shirt.
Step 2: Make a post on X answering the question: "Why Binance community is always at the forefront of the market".
Step 3: Add the hashtag #BinanceBlockchainWeek to your post.
Step 4: Share your post in this Binance Survey.
Binance will select the best 25 posts at its sole discretion. Winners of these 25 posts will share a prize pool of 500 USDC in token vouchers.
