# Introducing Binance P2P Academy Latam Program

This is a general announcement. Products and services mentioned may not be available in your region.
Fellow Binancians,
We are launching the end-of-year Binance P2P Academy Latam, a six-week educational program designed to help P2P users in LATAM grow their operations, deepen their knowledge of Binance P2P, and unlock advanced features. Participants who complete the program and its challenges can get access to an exclusive pool of rewards to be disclosed week by week during each live session. 
Campaign Period: 2026-11-20 00:00 (UTC) to 2026-12-31 23:59 (UTC)
How to Participate
Step 1. Enroll in the Bootcamp. Fill out the form, using an account that has completed KYC verification.
Step 2. Attend the weekly sessions. Join the live sessions on Binance Square and the Binance official channels.
Step 3. Complete the weekly challenges as announced.
Step 4. Reach the Leaderboard. Participants who progress through the program and complete the challenges are ranked on the Graduation Leaderboard to get an exclusive invitation to our End-of-Year event in Santa Cruz de Bolivia in December 2026.
