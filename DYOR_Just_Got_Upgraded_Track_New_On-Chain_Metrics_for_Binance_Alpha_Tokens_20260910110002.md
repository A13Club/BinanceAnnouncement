# DYOR Just Got Upgraded: Track New On-Chain Metrics for Binance Alpha Tokens

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

Binance has introduced an upgrade to DYOR, the dedicated research hub for Binance Alpha tokens, bringing even more on-chain transparency and project insights into a single interface.

Building on the initial launch, this latest iteration expands DYOR with deeper market intelligence, helping users research tokens more efficiently without switching between multiple analytics platforms.

What's New

This update introduces several new research capabilities:

1. Top Holders

Gain greater transparency into token ownership by understanding who holds a token and how holdings are distributed across major entities.

View entity-labeled holders (such as exchanges and DEX wallets)Analyze token concentration among major holdersJump directly to on-chain explorers for further verification

2. DEX Liquidity

Gain deeper visibility into liquidity across decentralized exchanges.

Compare liquidity across supported blockchainsMonitor hourly and daily liquidity trendsTrack liquidity drawdowns over time

3. CEX Wallet Sweep Movements

Track large token deposits from on-chain deposit addresses to centralized exchange wallets. 

View large exchange deposits from the sweep activityTrace source wallet addresses on-chainFollow transaction activity with direct explorer links

4. Project Fundamentals

Evaluate whether a project is actively building, with access to:

Team member informationOfficial X and LinkedIn profilesProject performance, such as TVL, fees and revenues.

5. Binance Open Interest & Funding Rate

Analyze spot market data alongside derivatives market data.

Monitor Open Interest trendsTrack Funding RatesCombine futures and spot insights for a more complete market view

Built on Trusted Data

DYOR brings these insights together into one research experience by aggregating publicly available data from trusted sources including Arkham, CoinMarketCap (CMC), DefiLlama, RootData, X, GitHub, and Binance's own public data. 

This helps users evaluate Binance Alpha tokens with greater transparency and efficiency.

How to Access DYOR

DYOR is available for supported Binance Alpha tokens.

To access it:

Open the Binance App and make sure the Exchange toggle is enabled at the top.Navigate to the Spot Trading page of a supported Alpha token. Tap on the [DYOR] tab.

We will gradually expand coverage to more tokens in the future.
