Important Notes:
Users' rewards depend on their eligible U, New U and Trading U relative to those of all eligible users, and other factors. The effective APRs will be published in this announcement after each weekly distribution.Sub-accounts trading volumes are not counted as separate entries under this campaign. Sub-account balances and trading volume are aggregated at the master account level, and rewards are distributed only to the master account. U borrowed through Margin or Loans (including interest), and any negative U balance in USDⓈ-M Futures, are treated as U liabilities and deducted from users’ U.The same eligible accounts apply to all stablecoins. When checking net borrowing of stablecoins (USDT, USDC, USD1, RLUSD, FDUSD or XUSD), and when measuring holdings of USDC, USD1, RLUSD, FDUSD and XUSD, only balances in the eligible accounts above are counted.For borrowed stablecoins (USDT, USDC, USD1, RLUSD, FDUSD and XUSD, including interest), the deduction is calculated for each coin separately. For example, if a user borrows 10,000 USDT and still holds 6,000 USDT, then 4,000 USDT will be deducted. Each liability is deducted only once across all their eligible accounts.New U is measured against users’ own average over the 3 weeks before the current week. This reference amount is updated daily. If the user’s combined holdings of USDC, USD1, RLUSD, FDUSD and XUSD fall below their combined 3-week average, that drop is deducted from their New U.The Trading Bonus applies for the full week once the user’s cumulative U-pair trading volume reaches 2,500 U-equivalent within that campaign week. Trading volume is counted in U-equivalent, with both buy and sell sides counted. U-versus-stablecoin pairs (USDT, USDC, USD1, RLUSD, FDUSD and XUSD) are excluded.At any snapshot, an eligible U balance below 0.01 U will be counted as zero.Rewards distributed are rounded down to 2 decimal places. There is no individual cap on rewards. Kindly note that the distribution time is not guaranteed and may change from time to time.If Binance identifies any abuse of the campaign rules, the related rewards will be cancelled, and Binance may disqualify the account from the campaign.
