Open your Binance App, tap [Add Funds], and select [Buy with USD].Switch to the [Recurring] tab in the top-right corner.Choose your desired token and purchase it using your USD balance. If you do not have sufficient balance, tap [+] to top up.Set the recurring purchase amount, frequency, and target account, then confirm and proceed.
