Fellow Binancians,
Binance Wallet will soon launch the Anthropic IPO xStocks Campaign. Eligible users can apply to participate in a non-guaranteed subscription process for Anthropic’s tokenized stocks via xStocks.
The IPO Campaign is designed to connect traditional capital markets with on-chain financial markets, enabling eligible users to submit subscription applications to be allocated Anthropic tokenized stocks upon Anthropic’s potential IPO. The role of Binance Wallet is limited to making the Campaign interface available to users and transmitting users’ subscription instructions to those parties.
Users who are successfully allocated will receive Anthropic tokenized stocks via xStocks after the issuance is completed, thereby obtaining price exposure to the Anthropic IPO. Please note that there is no guarantee that the user’s subscription application will be successful, as this will be determined by a number of factors. If the user’s subscription is not successful, the pledged funds will be refunded in full.
