# Introducing Gacha on Binance Wallet: Open Packs and Reveal Real, Graded Trading Cards

Disclaimer: This is a general announcement and marketing communication. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. Terms and conditions apply. 
Fellow Binancians,
Binance Wallet has integrated access to the Renaiss Protocol to launch Gacha, a pack-opening feature that brings collectible trading cards to your Binance Wallet. Pay a set price to open a pack and reveal a real, professionally graded physical card. What you hold is the on-chain digital certificate of that physical card, verifiable on BNB Smart Chain.
Key Highlights:
Real cards, professionally graded: Physical cards are authenticated and graded by professional third-party grading companies (following widely used standards such as PSA and CGC) and stored by a custodian in Renaiss Protocol's vault network.Published odds, open rules: Before opening, each pack page shows the full card pool, the odds for each rarity tier, and the complete rules.Verifiable fairness: Before the first pull of each Set, a cryptographic fingerprint of the card list (Merkle Root) is recorded on-chain, so the pool cannot be changed without detection. Results are generated by a Verifiable Random Function (VRF), and every pull comes with a proof that anyone can verify using public data.On-chain ownership: Each card is represented by an NFT on BNB Smart Chain, viewable through BscScan.Community Offer: Within 72 hours of opening, you can sell a card back at the price quoted. Cards not sold back are transferred to your Binance Wallet when the window closes.
How to Get Started:
Step 1. Update your Binance App to the latest version and open Binance Wallet.Step 2. Go to the [Markets] page and tap [Gacha].Step 3. Browse packs and review the card pool, odds, and full rules.Step 4. Choose a pack, confirm payment, and reveal your card. Up to 10 packs can be bought and opened at once.
Please Note:
Gacha currently runs on BNB Smart Chain (BSC), and packs are paid for with USDT from your Binance Wallet balance. Opening a pack is signature-free — no manual transaction confirmation is needed — but users should hold BNB in their wallet for gas fees.Subject to applicable laws, regulations and compliance requirements, Gacha may not be visible or available in some countries/regions or for some accounts.
