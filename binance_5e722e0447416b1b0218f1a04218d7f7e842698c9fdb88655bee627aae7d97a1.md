# Binance Completes Coverage of Assets Impacted in Security Incident; New Proof of Reserves Published

Fellow Binancians,
Binance has completed coverage of assets impacted in the security incident. Reflecting its ongoing commitment to transparency, Binance has also published a new Proof of Reserves (PoR) snapshot that provides an updated view of assets that are fully accounted for. 
Following the incident, Binance committed more than $6.34 billion in company funds to cover the losses and began sourcing affected tokens through market and OTC channels. That process was funded through a combination of a partial deployment of the Secure Asset Fund for Users (SAFU), a portion of Binance's Futures insurance fund (above the level required under Binance's risk-management framework), and Binance's own corporate treasury.
Binance has committed to replenishing SAFU to return the fund to approximately US$1 billion, ensuring it remains fully resourced as an emergency user-protection reserve. 
Proof of Reserves snapshot published
Binance has taken a new PoR snapshot at 2026-11-12 00:00 (UTC) that covers 198 tokens and is now available on the PoR website. Once the full zero-knowledge proof is generated — approximately 40 to 50 hours due to the computational workload — users holding the affected tokens will be able to independently verify that their assets are backed at a minimum of 1:1.
Withdrawals reopening in phases
Binance continues to reopen withdrawals in phases and will provide further updates as soon as they are confirmed.
Strengthening security and transparency
Security remains a top priority, and the company continues to invest heavily in its security infrastructure, with further enhancements ongoing across its platform.
Resolution regarding non-supported assets:
A very small number of tokens Binance no longer supports were affected. These will be handled according to our delisting process and users who hold them will be covered.
