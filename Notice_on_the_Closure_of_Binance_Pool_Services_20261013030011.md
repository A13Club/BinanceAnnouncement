# Notice on the Closure of Binance Pool Services

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. Terms and conditions apply. 

Fellow Binancians,

As part of our ongoing review of products and services, Binance will discontinue Binance Pool and related services on 2026-11-10. The services to be discontinued are Binance Pool, Pool Savings, Cloud Mining, Transaction Accelerator, as well as other Binance Pool-related features such as Telegram hashrate alerts, email notifications, and mining pool API services.

Timeline

2026-11-10 07:00 (UTC): All Binance Pool-related services will be discontinued. Binance Pool will begin disconnecting mining connections, and Binance Mining Pool Stratum URLs and mining endpoints will be disabled. Hashrate submissions and mining shares submitted after this time will no longer be accepted, processed, or credited. Binance Pool, Pool Savings, Cloud Mining, Transaction Accelerator, and other Binance Pool-related features will no longer be available, relevant product entry points will be closed, and no new rewards, yield, or service benefits will accrue after this time.

After 2026-11-10 07:00 (UTC): Eligible outstanding rewards, balances, refunds, or settlements, where applicable – will be processed, subject to applicable product rules, account status, risk controls, compliance checks, and applicable laws. Eligibility is determined in accordance with the applicable Mining Pool Product Terms.

Action Required

Users should take the following actions before the applicable discontinuation time:

Disconnect from Binance Pool before the applicable discontinuation time.Reconfigure your miners to point to a different pool before 2026-11-10 07:00 (UTC) to avoid interruptions after the cutoff.Please review your active Pool Savings positions and unsubscribe and/or redeem them before the service ends.Ensure your Binance account is able to receive any eligible remaining rewards, refunds, or settlements, including completing any required identity verification steps, where applicable. Please complete any necessary steps by 2026-11-10 23:59 (UTC) to facilitate timely processing.

Notes

Binance Pool is expected to continue operating under normal conditions until the applicable cutoff times listed above.Eligible mining shares submitted before 2026-11-10 07:00 (UTC) will be included in the final reward calculation in accordance with applicable Binance Pool rules.Download or save relevant records, reports, or transaction history, where needed.On 2026-11-10, all links to Binance Pool on the Binance homepage will be removed. The Binance Pool pages will remain accessible for 14 days, until 2026-11-24 07:00 (UTC) for viewing of historical data, account records, and payout status, where available, after which they will be permanently inaccessible.There may be discrepancies between this original content in English and any translated versions. Please refer to the original English version for the most accurate information, in case any discrepancies arise.

For questions or assistance, please contact Binance Customer Support or visit our Help Center.
