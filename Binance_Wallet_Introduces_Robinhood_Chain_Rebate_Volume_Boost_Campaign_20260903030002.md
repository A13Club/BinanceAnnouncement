# Binance Wallet Introduces Robinhood Chain Rebate Volume Boost Campaign

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

As trading activity on Robinhood Chain continues to grow, Binance Wallet is introducing the Robinhood Chain Rebate Volume Boost Campaign.

During the Campaign Period, referrers will receive a 2x boost on eligible Robinhood Chain trading volume. Trading volume generated by referrals on Robinhood Chain will count 2x toward the referrer's rebate tier calculation, helping referrers reach higher rebate tiers.

Campaign Period:

2026-09-03 00:00 (UTC) - 2026-09-18 23:59 (UTC)

How to Participate:

Share your Binance Wallet referral link and invite friends to register and use Binance Wallet.Referrals trade tokens on Robinhood Chain.Eligible trading volume will automatically count 2x toward the referrer's rebate tier calculation.Once the applicable threshold is reached, the referrer's rebate rate will be updated on T+1 day.

Examples:

Scenario 1: Rebate Tier Upgrade

During the Campaign Period, Referral A generates $80,000 in trading volume on Robinhood Chain.

With the 2x boost: $80,000 * 2 = $160,000

As the adjusted trading volume exceeds the $100,000 threshold, the referrer's rebate tier increases from 30% to 40%.

The referrer's subsequent rebate will be calculated based on the existing rebate system at the applicable 40% rebate rate.

Scenario 2: No Immediate Tier Upgrade

During the Campaign Period, Referrals A and B generate $30,000 and $40,000 in Robinhood Chain trading volume respectively.

With the 2x boost: ($30,000 + $40,000) * 2 = $140,000

As $140,000 does not reach the $200,000 threshold, the referrer remains at the 40% rebate tier.

If any of the referrals generate an additional $40,000 in Robinhood Chain trading volume, the additional volume counted will be: $40,000 * 2 = $80,000

Bringing the total adjusted trading volume to $220,000, which qualifies the referrer for the 50% rebate tier.
