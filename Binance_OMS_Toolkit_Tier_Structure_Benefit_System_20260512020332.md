# Binance OMS Toolkit Tier Structure & Benefit System

This is a general Binance Exchange Notice. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

To qualify for rebates of up to 50% as outlined in the table below, Binance Link clients must meet the required threshold for both average monthly trading volume and the average number of active trading users over a quarterly period. 

If a Binance Link client does not meet the minimum requirement(s) for the Bronze tier during a given quarter, the Binance Link client will be disqualified from the program and will no longer be eligible to receive rebates.

Notes:

The existing tiers for Binance Link clients, which are based on the average monthly trading volume in a quarter and the average number of monthly trading users in a quarter, will be automatically adjusted. Please note that Binance Link clients will be eligible to receive spot OMS toolkit rebates from spot trades completed by their regular and VIP 1-2 users only. On top of that, Binance Link clients will only receive futures OMS toolkit rebates from USDⓈ-M futures trades and COIN-M futures trades completed by their regular and VIP 1-2 users. Tiers and the corresponding benefits will be adjusted at the beginning of each quarter.
