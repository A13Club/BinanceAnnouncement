This is a general announcement and marketing communication. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

Binance will support the Chiliz fan tokens contract swap on Chiliz Chain (CAP20).

These tokens are:

AC Milan Fan Token (ACM)AS Roma Fan Token (ASR)Atlético de Madrid Fan Token (ATM)FC Barcelona Fan Token (BAR)Manchester City Fan Token (CITY)Juventus Fan Token (JUV)OG Fan Token (OG)Paris Saint-Germain Fan Token (PSG)

General

Deposits and WithdrawalsAt 2026-05-11 01:00 (UTC), deposits and withdrawals of the aforementioned tokens on Chiliz Chain (CAP20) will be suspended. Users should ensure they leave sufficient time for the aforementioned tokens’ deposits to be fully processed prior to this time. Binance will make a separate announcement to inform users after we resume deposits and withdrawals of the aforementioned tokens.After the event is complete, old ACM, ASR, ATM, BAR, CITY, JUV, OG, and PSG tokens will assume the ticker of ACMOLD, ASROLD, ATMOLD, BAROLD, CITYOLD, JUVOLD, OGOLD, PSGOLD respectively.Spot, Margin, and Futures trading and Binance Earn services will not be impacted during the contract swap.Binance will handle all technical requirements for users who are involved in this event.

Contract Swap

The aforementioned old Chiliz fan tokens will be swapped to new Chiliz fan tokens at a ratio of 1:1.
