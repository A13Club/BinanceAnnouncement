Ukrainian hryvnia (UAH), Kazakhstani tenge (KZT), Kyrgyzstani som (KGS), Georgian lari (GEL), Azerbaijani manat (AZN), Armenian dram (AMD), Tajikistani somoni (TJS), Belarusian ruble (BYN)

Competition 1: Be the Top 100 Growth Merchants to Share 20,000 USDT in Token Vouchers

Merchants who grow their trading volume by more than 20% compared to the previous month can qualify for this Competition.

All qualified merchants will be ranked by their month-over-month trading volume growth rate, where the top 100 merchants on the leaderboard will each receive a 200 USDT token voucher reward.

Note: If a merchant’s trading volume in the previous month is less than 50,000 USDT, they are not eligible to participate in Competition 1.

Competition 2: Grow Month-over-Month Volume to Share 20,000 USDT in Token Vouchers

Merchants who grow their trading volume by 25,000 USDT equivalent or more compared to the previous month can qualify for this Competition. Qualified merchants will be ranked by their total trading volume growth amount during the Activity Period. The top 50 merchants with the highest volume growth will receive rewards as per the table below.
