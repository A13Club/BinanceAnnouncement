# Binance Margin Will Initiate Routine Cleanup of Small-Balance Debt Accounts (2026-10-27)

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

Binance Margin will initiate a routine cleanup of Cross Margin accounts with outstanding liabilities valued at less than 1 USD (or equivalent in other assets), effective from 2026-10-27 00:00 (UTC). The cleanup will be executed on a daily basis as an ongoing operational process.

Target Accounts:

Cross Margin Accounts where the total outstanding liability is less than 1 USD equivalent across all assets.Margin Accounts with no capital activities in the past 6 months. Capital activity refers to any action that generates a capital flow, including but not limited to: Fund Transactions: Adding or removing funds to Margin Accounts;Borrowings / Repayments: Entering into margin loans or repaying margin loans; Close Position / Close All / Repay All: Using functions on the Margin trading page 

*Any open orders placed more than 6 months ago are not accounted as capital activity. 

Key Updates:

For each eligible account, the system will automatically deduct the corresponding liability amount of users’ margin loan(s) from the available assets in their Margin Accounts. If there are more than one type of available assets in the Margin Account, Binance will select the available asset type at its discretion. The Margin Account functions remain normal. Users can borrow or trade anytime after the cleanup. Accounts with total liabilities equal to or exceeding 1 USD equivalent are not affected by this cleanup.

Please Note:

The cleanup will be processed in batches.
