# Binance to Support 24/7 Dynamic Updates of the bStocks Collateral Index Price

Fellow Binancians,

To enhance the fairness and continuity of bStocks collateral valuation, Binance will update the bStocks collateral index price mechanism effective 2026-10-24 00:00 (UTC), enabling 24/7 dynamic updates. This will provide a continuous, dynamic price anchor for collateral valuation even during non-trading periods in the traditional regional markets, such as weekends and holidays.

Key Changes:

During non-trading hours (weekends, holidays, daily maintenance windows), the index price will reference Binance's trading prices and be formed using the Orderbook EWMA mode. To prevent abnormal price fluctuations during non-trading hours, the movement of the index price for collateral will be subject to a dedicated price movement cap. For more details, please refer to the bStocks Tokenized Securities Collateral Index Price Methodology.When exiting the orderbook EWMA mode, to prevent sudden price jumps, the Price Index transitions gradually using a weighted blend of the Price Index based on the previous calculation mode and the Price Index based on the new calculation mode.

Risk Disclosure:

Collateral value will be updated 24/7 along with the index price. The Margin Level or Unified Maintenance Margin Ratio (uniMMR) may change during non-trading hours. Please monitor your account status closely.Prices may change significantly when the market reopens after a non-trading period. Please manage your risks accordingly.Binance reserves the right to determine, at its sole discretion, what constitutes a "regular trading period" and may adjust the timeframe or calculation method of any trading session, as well as the Index Price Cap used for margin collateral valuation, at any time.Collateral ratios and related parameters may be adjusted at any time. Please refer to the Margin Data page for the most up-to-date information.
