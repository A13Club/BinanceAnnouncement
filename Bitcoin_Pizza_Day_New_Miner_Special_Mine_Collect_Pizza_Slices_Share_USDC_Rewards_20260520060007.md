Rewards Conversion:

After the Promotion Period ends, eligible users’ total Pizza Slices will be converted into USDC tokens.1 Pizza Slice is equivalent to 1 USDC reward.

Average Daily Hashrate Calculation:

Average Daily Hashrate = (User's Hashrate × Number of Days Mined) / 31 days

Note: The calculation is based on the full 31-day Promotion Period regardless of when a user begins mining. Users who start later will have a lower average daily hashrate.
