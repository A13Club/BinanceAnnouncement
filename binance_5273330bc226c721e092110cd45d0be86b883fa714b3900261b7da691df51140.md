# Early Completion of Contract Size Adjustment on USDⓈ-Margined BITOUSDT Perpetual Contract (2026-11-19)

Fellow Binancians,
Binance Futures will complete the contract size adjustment of USDⓈ-Margined BITOUSDT Perpetual Contract at 2026-11-19 02:10 (UTC). There will be a cancel-only phase which may last up to 5 minutes after the adjustment is complete. The trading function will resume at 2026-11-19 02:15 (UTC). 
Please Note:
It is highly recommended that users re-assess their collateral balances and positions in both Cross Margin and Isolated Margin Futures Accounts to avoid from being liquidated due to insufficient maintenance margin or price fluctuations that may occur. During the cancel-only phase, users are allowed to submit, modify or cancel Post-Only orders only. The duration of the cancel-only phase will be determined by Binance based on actual market liquidity and may last up to 5 minutes.At 2026-11-19 02:15 (UTC), the trading function resumes and Binance Futures will apply Last Price Protected (“LPP”) Mechanism on the aforementioned perpetual contract. It is highly recommended that users with open positions in the aforementioned perpetual contract re-assess their position before the trading is resumed to mitigate against price fluctuations that may occur.At 2026-11-19 14:30 (UTC), the LPP period of the aforementioned perpetual contract will end when a stable index price can be derived from third-party data vendors. The Mark Price will transition gradually from LPP mechanism to standard Mark Price calculation. The transition will be applied gradually by using mark price smoothing process to minimise sudden price movements and to better ensure price stability during the transition.The mark price and index price of the aforementioned perpetual contract before the adjustment will be retained and continued with the newly adjusted price after the adjustment is complete.The last price continuous Kline (candlestick) chart of the aforementioned perpetual contract will be corrected within 24 hours after the adjustment is complete. API users may use GET /fapi/v1/exchangeInfo and UI users can refer to this page to check the latest trading status of the perpetual contract during the adjustment process.
