Fellow Binancians,

Binance is launching the Futures Copy Trading Livestream Challenge! Eligible Futures lead traders can go live on Binance Square to climb the leaderboard based on their Copier PnL (total copy trader PNL) and share a prize pool of up to 15,000 USDT.

Eligibility:

All Futures lead traders who register for the activity can participate. If you’re not a Futures lead trader, complete identity verification and submit your application to become one during the Registration Period to be eligible.

How to Participate:

Select an existing public/private Futures Copy Trading portfolio or create a new portfolio to join the competition. Once submitted, the selected portfolio cannot be changed during the Trading Challenge Period.Complete the registration form and submit your Binance UID and the designated portfolio details during the Registration Period.Use the registered portfolio to participate in the competition after passing the eligibility review.

Note:

Each participant may submit one portfolio only. Smart Money Signal Copy Trading portfolios are not eligible for this activity.Users will receive a notification between 2026-08-27 and 2026-09-06 in Binance Square if they pass the eligibility review.

Competition Structure:

Participants will be ranked based on the participating portfolio’s Copier PnL (total copy trader PNL) during the Trading Challenge Period.

During the Trading Challenge Period, participants must trade using their registered portfolio. There are no additional rules to capital aside from the minimum lead trading amount. After the Trading Challenge Period ends, participants who meet all of the following requirements and rank in the top 50 by Copier PnL (total copy trader PNL), from highest to lowest, will share the 10,000 USDT base prize pool.

Requirements:

The participating portfolio has ≥ 50 copiers during the Trading Challenge Period;The participating portfolio remains active for ≥ 7 days during the Trading Challenge Period;The participating portfolio’s Copier PnL (total copy trader PNL) is > 0 during the Trading Challenge Period.
