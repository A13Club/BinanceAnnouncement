# SAFU Update: Adjusting Fund Holdings

Fellow Binancians, 
The Secure Asset Fund for Users (SAFU) is an emergency insurance fund that Binance established in July 2018 to protect users’ assets. The SAFU fund offers protection to Binance users and their funds in the event of extreme situations.
We are replenishing the fund and updating how SAFU is held:
We are making a change to how SAFU is held. 7,500 BTC will move out of the SAFU address back to Binance's own treasury account. In exchange, 500 million USDC from Binance's own treasury account will be added to SAFU.
This is an internal reallocation, not a sale.No BTC will be sold or traded on the market. This is an internal transfer between SAFU and Binance's own funds. It has no market impact.
Why we are doing it: 
This change meets a regulatory requirement. In addition, a larger stablecoin share makes SAFU more stable and immediately usable if it is ever needed.
SAFU's purpose remains the same: To protect users in an extreme event. Binance's commitment to replenish SAFU is also unchanged. SAFU remains held and managed by Nest Clearing and Custody Limited, an ADGM-regulated Recognised Clearing House. You can verify the movements on-chain at the SAFU addresses.
Thank you for your support!
