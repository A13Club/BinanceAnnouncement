# Early Completion of Contract Size Adjustment on USDⓈ-Margined SNDKUSDT Perpetual Contract (2026-11-20)

Fellow Binancians,
Binance Futures will complete the contract size adjustment of USDⓈ-Margined SNDKUSDT Perpetual Contract at 2026-11-20 02:30 (UTC). There will be a cancel-only phase which may last up to 15 minutes after the adjustment is complete. The trading function will resume at 2026-11-20 02:45 (UTC). 
Please Note:
During the Adjustment Period, users with open positions on the aforementioned perpetual contract will be closed first. Unrealized PNL will be settled and credited to the users’ Cross Margin Futures Account regardless of the users’ position mode setting. For the users with open positions in isolated mode, the realized PNL and all margin balance in the Isolated Margin Futures Account will be transferred back to the Cross Margin Futures Account. After the adjustment is complete, new positions will be re-instated. The margin balance that will be transferred by the system to the Isolated Margin Futures Account is based on the initial margin requirement of the leverage level users have selected before the adjustment. If the available balance in the Cross Margin Futures Account is lower than the initial margin requirement needed to re-instate the new positions during the funds transfer process, the system will transfer the available balance from the Cross Margin Futures Account. Thus, it is highly recommended that users re-assess their collateral balances and positions in both Cross Margin and Isolated Margin Futures Accounts to avoid from being liquidated due to insufficient maintenance margin or price fluctuations that may occur. During the cancel-only phase, users are allowed to submit, modify or cancel Post-Only orders only. The duration of the cancel-only phase will be determined by Binance based on actual market liquidity and may last up to 15 minutes.At 2026-11-20 02:45 (UTC), the trading function resumes and Binance Futures will apply Last Price Protected (“LPP”) Mechanism on the aforementioned perpetual contract. It is highly recommended that users with open positions in the aforementioned perpetual contract re-assess their position before the trading is resumed to mitigate against price fluctuations that may occur to avoid liquidation. Relatively extreme price fluctuations may occur due to limited liquidity during the LPP period. The last price of the aforementioned perpetual contract during the LPP period may be deviated from the adjusted mark price after the share splits due to unavailability of spot price in non-trading hours. Under illiquid market conditions, the orders may not be fully executed or expired when the Limit/Market Order Price Cap/Floor Ratio surpasses a specific threshold (PERCENT_PRICE filter limit). The system will reject any outstanding orders, and there will be an error message stating that the counterparty's best price falls outside the PERCENT_PRICE filter limit. Users are advised to exercise caution and evaluate risk when trading during the LPP period. Binance will not be liable for any losses the users may incur due to price fluctuations or expired/unfilled orders. Please note that stricter minimum and maximum prices will be imposed during the LPP period. The minimum and maximum prices are set at ±10% from the adjusted mark price. For example, if the adjusted mark price is 100 USDT, the minimum price will be 90 USDT while the maximum price will be 110 USDT. Binance may adjust the minimum and maximum prices from time to time based on market risk conditions. API users may use GET /fapi/v1/exchangeInfo while UI users can refer to this page to find the latest minimum and maximum prices.Binance reserves the right to impose temporary “Reduce Only” restrictions from time to time on the aforementioned perpetual contract during the LPP period.At 2026-11-20 14:30 (UTC), the LPP period of the aforementioned perpetual contract will end when a stable index price can be derived from third-party data vendors. The Mark Price will transition gradually from LPP mechanism to standard Mark Price calculation. The transition will be applied gradually by using mark price smoothing process to minimise sudden price movements and to better ensure price stability during the transition.The mark price and index price of the aforementioned perpetual contract before the adjustment will be retained and continued with the newly adjusted price after the adjustment is complete.The last price continuous Kline (candlestick) chart of the aforementioned perpetual contract will be corrected within 24 hours after the adjustment is complete. API users may use GET /fapi/v1/exchangeInfo and UI users can refer to this page to check the latest trading status of the perpetual contract during the adjustment process.
