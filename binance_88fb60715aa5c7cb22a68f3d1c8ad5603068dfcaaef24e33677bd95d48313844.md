# Introducing Lowered Binance U.S. Stock Trading Fees With VIP Tiers - 2026-11-25

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.
Fellow Binancians,
To further optimize the stock trading experience, Binance will adjust the Spread Fee and Platform Fee for stock trading starting from 2026-11-25 08:00 (UTC).
Updated Fees Schedule: 
VIP LevelSpread Platform Fee (USD)Regular User - VIP 30.05%0.17VIP 4 - VIP 70.04%0.17VIP 8 - VIP 90.01%0.17
For each order, either the applicable Platform Fee or Spread Fee will be charged, whichever is higher.
Spread Fee
The Spread will be categorized into three tiers ranging from 0.01% to 0.05%, depending on the user’s VIP level. 
Platform Fee
The Platform Fee will be reduced from 0.35 USD to 0.17 USD.
Examples:
User A is a Regular User with a 0.05% Spread Fee, the following fees will apply.
If User A completes a trade of 1,000 USDC:
Spread Fee = 1,000 × 0.05% = 0.50 USDC.As the Spread Fee is higher than the 0.17 USDC Platform Fee, the applicable fee will be 0.50 USDC.
If User A completes a trade of 200 USDC:
Spread Fee = 200 × 0.05% = 0.10 USDC.As the spread fee is lower than the 0.17 USDC Platform Fee, the applicable fee will be 0.17 USDC.
Notes:
The new fee rate will be available on iOS app version 3.21.0 and above, and Android app version 3.21.0 and above.Fees charged are rounded up to two decimal places.Please refer to the Fees Page for the latest applicable fee display.There may be discrepancies between this original content in English and any translated versions.
