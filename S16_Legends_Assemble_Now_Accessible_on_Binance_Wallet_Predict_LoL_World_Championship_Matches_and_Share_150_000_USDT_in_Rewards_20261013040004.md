Get Started

How to Participate:

Step 1: Enter with 10 USDT and build your winning streak

Before each match begins, place an eligible prediction of at least 10 USDT on the League of Legends World Championship 2026 using your own funds and hold your position until market settlement.

Earn up to 32 Match Points per match through consecutive correct predictions, plus additional Participation Streak Points for continued participation.

Step 2: Create a team and unlock bonus points

Create or join a team consisting of 1-5 eligible members to compete on the team leaderboard and earn team bonus of up to +100% on Match Points.

Step 3: Compete on two leaderboards

Weekly Individual Leaderboard: Compete across the first three weekly rounds for individual rewards.Team Leaderboard: Compete for a share of the 150,000 USDT total team reward pool.

Distribution Within a Winning Team:

Each team reward will be allocated among its eligible members in proportion to each member’s individual score credited to that team.

Member Reward = Team Reward * Member’s Credited Score / Total Credited Score of All Eligible Team Members

Match Points earned before activation are not included in a member’s credited team score. Favorite-team points earned before activation are included once the member becomes eligible. A member with zero credited team score does not participate in the allocation; a winning one-member team receives the full team reward.
