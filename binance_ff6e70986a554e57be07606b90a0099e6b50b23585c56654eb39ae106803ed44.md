# Introducing Binance Pay Later: Spend Without Selling Your Crypto and Enjoy 0% Interest Until the First of Next Month

This is a general announcement and marketing communication. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. Terms and conditions apply.
Fellow Binancians,
Binance Pay is excited to introduce Pay Later, a new crypto-backed spending feature that allows eligible users to make purchases through Binance Card or QR Pay without selling their crypto holdings.
With Binance Pay Later, eligible users can access a spending limit of up to 1,000 USDC, backed by their BTC, ETH or BNB holdings. Instead of borrowing the full amount upfront, users only borrow what they spend, when they spend it.
Users can also enjoy 0% interest until the 1st of the following calendar month, allowing them to make purchases and repay by the due date without incurring interest.
Binance Pay Later will initially be available to eligible users in Argentina and Mexico, with more markets to follow.
Key Benefits of Pay Later:
Spending without selling crypto: Access spending power backed by your BTC, ETH, or BNB holdings, with a spending limit of up to 50% of your eligible collateral value, capped at 1,000 USDC per user.0% interest until the due date: Make purchases now and pay no interest when you repay by the 1st of the following calendar month.Borrow as you spend: Access credit directly when making a purchase, without taking out a separate loan upfront. Only the transaction amount is borrowed.Pay with Binance Card or QR: Make purchases with your Binance Card or scan QR codes to pay at participating merchants, locally or abroad.3% cashback: Earn up to 3% cashback on eligible Binance Card purchases, subject to applicable reward terms.
How to Use Pay Later:
Step 1. Access Pay Later: Open the Binance App, navigate to [Binance Pay], and tap [Pay Later], subject to eligibility and availability. Ensure that you completed identity verification.Step 2. Get your spending limit: Access a spending limit of up to 1,000 USDC, backed by BTC, ETH or BNB.Step 3. Make a purchase: Use Binance Pay Later through Binance Card or supported QR payments. The corresponding amount is borrowed in USDC, and the required crypto collateral is locked at the time of payment.Step 4. Repay by the due date: Repay the outstanding amount by the 1st of the following calendar month to enjoy 0% interest.
Important Notes:
The maximum spending limit is 1,000 USDC, subject to eligibility and applicable product conditions.Interest is 0% until the repayment due date. Interest charges apply to overdue amounts.
