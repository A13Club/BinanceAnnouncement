# Important Notice on PREDICT Airdrop Token Allocation

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. Terms and conditions apply. 
Fellow Binancians, 
As the launch of Predict (PREDICT) approaches, users who hold Predict Points (“PP Points”) may now prepare for the airdrop claim process.
PP Points are points earned by users on the Predict platform through trading, providing effective liquidity, holding positions, and early participation in markets. If you have earned PP Points, you may view them through either of the following paths:
Path 1：Binance - Assets - Prediction AccountPath 2：Binance Wallet - Discover - dApp - Predict - Assets
Actions to Take:
If you are eligible for the airdrop, please submit the official form to indicate your preferred claim option. The submission deadline is 2026-11-26 09:00 (UTC). 
You may choose one of the following two options. If no submission is made before the deadline, Option B will apply by default. Each user may submit the form only once, and no changes can be made after submission.
Option A: Claim Immediately
You may receive 35% of your entitled token allocation. By choosing Option A, you automatically forfeit the remaining 65% of the tokens. Once forfeited, this portion cannot be recovered from the token project.
If you choose Option A and wish to receive your tokens on the day PREDICT goes live, please submit this form before 2026-10-29 09:00 (UTC). 
If you submit Option A after this time, your tokens will be included in a subsequent weekly release batch. The release dates are 2026-11-06, 2026-11-13, 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-27. The cutoff time for each batch is 09:00 (UTC) on the day before the relevant release date.
Option B: Claim After One Year of Staking
After the token project completes one year of staking, you may receive 100% of your entitled token allocation.
Token Allocation
Depending on where your PP Points are held, the tokens will be released to different accounts:  
Path 1: The corresponding tokens will be credited to your Binance Alpha account.  Path 2: The corresponding tokens will be credited to your Binance Wallet.
