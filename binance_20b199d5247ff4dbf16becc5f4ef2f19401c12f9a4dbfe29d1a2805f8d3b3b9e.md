# Binance Futures Updates Trading Fee Discount for TradFi Perps (2026-11-04)

This is a general announcement and marketing communication. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.
Fellow Binancians,
On 2026-10-28, Binance announced that the TradFi Perps taker fee discount would be reduced from 50% to 40% for VIP 4 to 9 users with effect from 2026-11-05 08:00 (UTC).
That change will not be implemented. The 50% taker fee discount will continue to apply until further notice.
This notice supersedes the announcement of 2026-10-28. 
The promotion announced on 2026-03-31 therefore continues on its original terms. Regular to VIP 3 users will continue to receive a 20% discount on taker fees for TradFi Perps, unchanged and unaffected by this notice. VIP 4 to 9 users will continue to receive a 50% discount on taker fees for TradFi Perps, compared to the standard USDT taker fee rate.
Promotion Period: 2026-03-31 02:00 (UTC) until further notice
