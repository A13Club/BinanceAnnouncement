This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region. Terms and conditions apply.

Fellow Binancians,

Binance Wallet is excited to introduce the Prediction Markets Trial Protection Campaign!  Register and get up to 10 USDT in loss coverage on your first eligible prediction.

Promotion Period: 2026-05-07 14:00 (UTC) to 2026-05-14 14:00 (UTC)

During the Promotion Period, the first 10,000 eligible users who successfully register for the campaign may receive up to 10 USDT in loss protection* if their first Prediction Markets order meets all of the following criteria:

The user successfully registers for the campaign during the Promotion Period;The order is placed after registration and during the Promotion Period; andThe market resolves by 2026-05-15 14:00 (UTC), and the order resolves at a loss.

How to Participate

Step 1: Register for the Campaign

During the Promotion Period, visit the Campaign Page and tap [Register] to complete registration.

Step 2: Access Prediction Markets (Three Entry Points)

Entry Point 1 — Binance Exchange: Update your Binance App to the latest version (iOS 3.11.1+, Android 3.11.2+).Log in to your Binance App, enter [Exchange], navigate to [Markets], and tap [Prediction] (only visible in supported regions).First-time users will be prompted to create a [Prediction Account] powered by Binance Wallet keyless technology.

Entry Point 2 — Binance Wallet DApp Browser: Open your Binance App, switch to [Wallet], and tap [Home] at the bottom navigation bar.On the home page, scroll to the [Prediction] section and open Predict.fun in the dApp browser via Binance Wallet (Keyless).

Entry Point 3 — Binance Wallet Extension:Visit Predict.fun and click [Log In] or [Sign Up].Select Binance Wallet (Keyless) to log in. First-time users need to scan the QR code with their Binance App; existing Binance Wallet Extension users can simply sign the message to confirm.

Step 3: Place Your First Trade

Select a prediction event and your outcome, choose a Market Order or Limit Order, enter the amount, and place your order in USDT.How to fund:Exchange: Orders default to your Spot or Funding Account balance, but you can choose to pay with your Prediction Account balance if available.DApp (Predict.fun): Before placing your first order, tap [Deposit] to fund with USDT on BNB Smart Chain.
