What Disqualifies Engagement from Counting

Only genuine engagement counts. The following will not be counted and may result in disqualification from the program:

Manufactured engagement

Coordinated engagement, including mutual-engagement rings, like-for-like groups, and follow-for-follow groups, will not be counted. Engagement from non-genuine accounts, such as bulk-registered, alternate, or sockpuppet accounts, will also be excluded. Engagement between related accounts, including accounts linked by the same master account, shared or related devices, IP addresses, or fund flows, will not be counted. Engagement from accounts showing abnormally high-frequency or mechanical activity will also be excluded.

Fake engagement (not backed by genuine reading behavior)

Engagement must be supported by genuine browsing and reading behavior. Likes, comments, or follows that occur without meaningful content consumption, or that clearly deviate from normal user behavior, will not be counted. The platform also evaluates the natural interaction history between a creator and engaging accounts. One-off or concentrated engagement without sustained, organic interaction may be treated as non-genuine. Any scripted, programmatic, manipulated, inorganic, coordinated, or abusive engagement will not be counted.

Other excluded engagement

Withdrawn engagement, including removed likes, unfollows, and deleted comments, will not be counted. Repeated engagement is also subject to de-duplication: a reader’s like or comment on the same content counts only once, and following of the same creator counts only once per week.

Binance calculates each qualified creator‘s commission rewards at the end of each week, and will distribute the weekly rewards in USDC to their Spot Account by the following Thursday at 23:59 (UTC). Each week runs from Monday 00:00 (UTC) to Sunday 23:59 (UTC).
