Fellow Binancians,
Thank you for applying for a subscription in the Anthropic IPO xStocks campaign made available through Binance Wallet. The IPO allocation received for this campaign was not ideal relative to total user commitments. As a thank-you for taking part, the allocation will be distributed to all eligible participants at no cost to them, in proportion to the amount each committed. All committed USDC will be returned in full even if the user receives an allocation, and eligible participants who applied for a subscription will receive an additional airdrop as appreciation for their enthusiasm. 
Details below:
1. Full Return
All locked USDC from participating users will be fully returned to their Binance Wallet via the original payment method. No action is required on users’ part. 
2. Airdrop of Allocated Shares
The shares allocated to this campaign will be airdropped at no cost to eligible participants. Eligible users will receive Anthropic tokenized stocks pro-rata to the total amount they each subscribed and it will be airdropped directly to their Binance Wallet addresses by 2026-11-20.
3. $1,000,000 ANTB Airdrop
Recognizing the strong enthusiasm across the community for Anthropic IPO xStocks campaign, as a gesture of appreciation for eligible participants, a total of $1,000,000 worth of bStocks Anthropic tokens (ANTB) will be shared with all users who applied to participate in this campaign. Each eligible user will receive a minimum of 6 USDC worth of ANTB, with the remaining pool distributed on a pro-rata basis, based on each user's subscription amount relative to the total subscription amount of all participating users. The airdrop will be automatic and viewable in eligible users’ Binance Exchange Spot Accounts by 2026-11-20. No action is required.
Please note that airdrops are priced based on Anthropic's market price after stable trading begins on Nasdaq. The actual value received may fluctuate with market conditions.
What Is ANTB:
ANTB is an upcoming bStocks token - a digital asset designed to track the price of Anthropic shares, intended to be fully backed by real Anthropic shares held by a regulated custodian, with publicly verifiable proof of reserves. 
ANTB will soon be available for trading on Binance Spot — stay tuned.
