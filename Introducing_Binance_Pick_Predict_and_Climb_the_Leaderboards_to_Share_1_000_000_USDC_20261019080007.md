How It Works:

Step 1. Join the Campaign 

Visit the Binance Pick campaign page and select [Join Now]. Reserve during Early Bird to earn bonus Scores. Eligible new users who join the campaign earn additional bonus Scores.

Step 2. Complete Tasks to Earn Scores

Complete the tasks available to you to earn Scores. Tasks include Join Campaign / Early Bird reservation, New User Welcome, referrals, daily check-in, Spot/Convert/Futures Trading Task, post on Square and the Refill Station.

Step 3. Use Your Scores to Predict in Binance Pick

For users who are from an eligible jurisdiction, use your Scores to join available markets in Binance Pick. Close your position before it ends or hold until settlement. A correct prediction increases your Scores.

Please note that Scores are campaign-only points used only for campaign ranking and reward allocation. They have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed, transferred, or exchanged for cash. Scores reset at the end of the season under the Campaign Terms.

Step 4. Climb the Leaderboard and Win Rewards

Track your ranking on the Leaderboard page. Rewards are distributed across three pools:

Season Leaderboard — 500,000 USDC: The top 20,000 participants on the leaderboard after the campaign ends can share rewards. Participants must have more than 0 Season Scores to be eligible.The Season Leaderboard closes on 2026-11-15 at 23:59:59 (UTC).Weekly Leaderboard — 400,000 USDC in total (100,000 USDC/week): The top 8,000 participants on the leaderboard at the end of each weekly ranking period can share rewards. Participants must have a Weekly PnL greater than 0 from predictions made with Scores. The Weekly Leaderboard closes every Sunday at 23:59:59 (UTC).Participation Reward — 100,000 USDC: Participants who are not ranked on the Season Leaderboard or any Weekly Leaderboard and have completed at least 5 check-ins during the season can share rewards, with a maximum of 5 USDC per eligible participant.
