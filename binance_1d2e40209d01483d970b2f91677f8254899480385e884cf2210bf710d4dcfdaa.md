# Binance Margin System Maintenance: Certain Order Types Will Be Cancelled (2026-12-18)

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.
Fellow Binancians,
Binance Margin will perform system maintenance from 2026-12-18 07:00 (UTC) to 10:00 (UTC). During the maintenance period, open orders of the following types placed by Regular Users on Binance Margin may be cancelled. These order types will resume normal operation once the maintenance is complete. Other order types and Margin Account functions are not affected.
Affected Order Types: 
Stop Loss and Take Profit Market OrderStop Loss and Take Profit Orders with Trailing DeltaBBO ordersOCO/OTO/OPO/OTOCO/OPOCO orders Auto Borrow/Auto Repay orders
Targeted Accounts:
All Regular Users on Binance Margin, including Cross Margin, Isolated Margin and Portfolio Margin. 
Please Note:
We strongly recommend that you review your open orders in advance. If you hold open orders of the aforementioned affected types, please cancel or place other order types  according to your own trading needs before 2026-12-18 07:00 (UTC), and monitor your order status throughout the maintenance period.Please monitor your Margin Account and positions closely during the maintenance period, and manage your positions and risk on your own.Cancelled orders do not affect your assets or liabilities. Your assets and liabilities remain unchanged.Binance is not liable for any losses incurred as a result of the cancellation of the above order types or the unavailability of these order types during the maintenance period.There may be discrepancies between this original content in English and any translated versions.
