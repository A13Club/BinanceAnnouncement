# Introducing Auto Order Routing for Spot Trading

This is a general Binance Exchange notice and a notice for the purposes of the Exchange rules. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

Binance has launched Auto Order Routing, a new feature that compares the order book of your local currency pair against a more liquid intermediary pair, and routes your order via whichever path offers the better quoted price. Starting today, U-quoted symbols now support Auto Order Routing, with USD, USD1, USDC, and other quote assets  to follow, subject to regulatory approvals in respective jurisdictions.

What Is Auto Order Routing?

For low-liquidity trading pairs, particularly those quoted in local fiat currencies, pricing can vary depending on available market depth. Binance's new Automatic Order Routing feature helps you access deeper liquidity with ease.

Previously, users had to execute trades in multiple manual steps, paying a trading fee at each stage, for example, converting U to USDT, then USDT to BTC. With this feature, the entire process runs automatically in the background. You pay just one trading fee at the applicable rate, deducted from the receiving asset of the executed pair (e.g., a BTC/U sell order routed via BTC/USDT is charged in USDT). If enabled, the BNB fee discount applies as usual. One click is all it takes to trade into your target asset through the optimal route.

Note: Price improvement is an opportunity, not a guarantee, as order books continue to change after the routing decision has been made.

Key Features

Better liquidity. When you place an order with Auto Order Routing enabled, Binance compares the local currency order book and the intermediary order book and routes your order via whichever path offers the better quoted price.No extra fees. You pay your standard Binance Spot trading fee based on your VIP tier and BNB discount status. No commission is charged by Binance on the intermediary quote currency conversion (e.g. USDT/U) forming part of the routed path, although standard spreads will apply.Market and limit orders both supported. This includes Limit GTC, Limit IOC, Market Sell denominated in the base currency, and Market Buy denominated in the quote currency.You stay in control. Auto Order Routing can be enabled or disabled via the “Trading Mode” dropdown on the trading page. When disabled, your order routes to the local currency pair only.

Availability

Auto Order Routing will be rolled out gradually on our platform. Trading pairs eligible for Auto Order Routing will be indicated as such on the trading interface. You should check if the relevant trading pair is eligible for Auto Order Routing before placing your order. 

Auto Order Routing may only be available to eligible users in certain countries. Auto Order Routing is not intended for users to which restrictions or prohibitions apply.
